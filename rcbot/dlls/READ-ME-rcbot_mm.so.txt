This .so is STOCK rcbot and does NOT contain the TS team fix.

On linux, bots go back to attacking their own team: stock rcbot decides
The Specialists teams by comparing player model strings, so teammates in
different models read as enemies.

To build one: clone rcbotold at the commit in ..\..\rcbot-patch\BASE-COMMIT.txt,
apply rcbot-ts-teams.patch, build with the AMBuildScript in the repo root.
