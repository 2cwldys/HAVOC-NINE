FFmpeg -- notice
================

H9XASH ships five FFmpeg shared libraries. The engine loads them by name at runtime, with
LoadLibrary, and uses them to decode AVI video: the intro, the main-menu background and the
logo banner.

    avutil-61.dll      libavutil 61
    avformat-63.dll    libavformat 63
    avcodec-63.dll     libavcodec 63
    swresample-7.dll   libswresample 7
    swscale-10.dll     libswscale 10

They sit in the root of H9XASH, beside the game, with a copy of this notice and of the licence
text as ffmpeg.NOTICE.txt and ffmpeg.LGPL-2.1-LICENSE.txt.


THE LICENCE
-----------
FFmpeg 9.0.2, under the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 2.1 or later. The full
text is THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES/FFmpeg-LGPL-2.1.txt in the repository, and
ffmpeg.LGPL-2.1-LICENSE.txt beside the libraries themselves.

FFmpeg can also be built under the GPL, and parts of it are GPL-only. This build is not one
of those. It was configured without --enable-gpl, without --enable-nonfree and without
--enable-version3, so it carries no GPL-only and no version-3 component, and the LGPL 2.1
applies to the whole of it.


NOT MODIFIED
------------
FFmpeg's own source was not changed. Every H9 change to the AVI path is in the engine that
CALLS FFmpeg -- xash3d-fwgs/engine/client/avi/avi_ffmpeg.c and the patches under
xash3d-h9-patches/patches -- and none of it is inside these libraries.


THE SOURCE, AND REPLACING THEM
------------------------------
FFmpeg 9.0.2's source is published by the FFmpeg project at https://ffmpeg.org/download.html
and in its own repository, https://git.ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg.git, at tag n9.0.2. These libraries
were built from that release, unchanged, with the i686 MinGW-w64 cross-toolchain under MSYS2
and this configure line:

    --target-os=mingw32 --arch=x86 --cross-prefix=i686-w64-mingw32-
    --ar=ar --nm=nm --ranlib=ranlib --strip=strip --windres=windres
    --extra-cflags=-mstackrealign
    --enable-shared --disable-static
    --disable-programs --disable-doc --disable-debug --disable-avdevice

docs/FFMPEG_ADDITIONS.txt records why each of those is there; --extra-cflags=-mstackrealign
in particular is not optional, and a build without it crashes the first time the MSVC-built
engine calls into it.

Anyone may rebuild these five libraries from that source and that line and drop the result in
beside the game. The engine loads them by name at runtime and links to none of them at build
time, so a replacement needs nothing of H9XASH rebuilt and no part of H9XASH relinked. That
is the freedom the LGPL asks for, and this build keeps it.


SIX LIBRARIES BESIDE THEM, WHICH ARE NOT FFmpeg
-----------------------------------------------
FFmpeg's own import tables name six more, so they ship too, and without them LoadLibrary on
avutil-61.dll fails and the whole AVI path goes dark with no error. They are not part of
FFmpeg and are not covered by the licence above; each keeps its own, from the project that
wrote it:

    libgcc_s_dw2-1.dll    GCC's runtime library, under the GPLv3 WITH the GCC Runtime
                          Library Exception, which is what allows it to be shipped here
    libwinpthread-1.dll   the mingw-w64 project's threads runtime, under its own
                          MIT-style permission notice
    libbz2-1.dll          bzip2, under the bzip2 licence (BSD-style)
    libiconv-2.dll        GNU libiconv, under the GNU LGPL version 2.1 or later -- the same
                          text as the one beside this file
    liblzma-5.dll         liblzma from XZ Utils, under the 0BSD licence
    zlib1.dll             zlib, under the zlib licence

Their full texts are published by each project, and MSYS2 carries them under
mingw32/share/licenses/<package>/ in the toolchain these were taken from.
