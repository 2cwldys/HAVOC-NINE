This section will explain to you how to use the grabtype command in Ent Mod:

Command Use:

  weapon_entmover

Usage:
weapon_entmover

Description:
Draws the entmover weapon that gives you a more precise, and in some cases, easier method of moving entities.

 

First, you will need to enter the weapon_entmover command in the console.

 

After the command has been entered you will now be holding the entmover gun.

 

Holding down your attack key will grab the entity and cause it to move wherever you look.

 

After you stop holding the attack key, the entity will be located in that position.

 

If you wish to delete an entity, press your secondary fire key (right mouse button by default) and it will be deleted.
