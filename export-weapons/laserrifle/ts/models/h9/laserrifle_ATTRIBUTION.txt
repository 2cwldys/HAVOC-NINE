LASER RIFLE: ATTRIBUTION
========================

The Laser Rifle view model (v_laserrifle.mdl), player model (p_laserrifle.mdl)
and world model (w_laserrifle.mdl) are adapted from a 3D model published on
Sketchfab:

  "Sci-Fi Marksman Rifle (Updated Textures)" (https://skfb.ly/ozONJ) by Jett
  Spaulding is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution
  (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

  Model:   https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/sci-fi-marksman-rifle-updated-textures-909c08e41bb3491bac9110d9cf1d48e4
  Author:  Jett Spaulding
  Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0). It
           allows use and adaptation, including commercial use, with credit
           to the author, a link to the licence and a note of what was
           changed (below).

CHANGES MADE FOR THE SPECIALISTS / H9XASH

  - The mesh (118,540 triangles in 21 parts) was brought down part by part to
    about 9,400 triangles (view model) and 4,500 (player and world models), to
    fit what a GoldSrc model holds. The flashlight parts and the optic's mount
    were left out, the optic was lowered onto the receiver (and made lower in
    the view model so that it does not stand up into the player's view), the
    magazine, which the file shows out of the rifle, was seated in its well,
    and the barrel's pieces were simplified on a grid.
  - The optic's two lenses were remade as blue hexagonal glass plates (the
    file's lens is red) and given a picture of their own.
  - The 1024 x 1024 physically based textures were baked into one colour
    picture of 512 x 512: the painted metal is white (the file's tan fill
    became white), the underside, grip and magazine black, the normal maps
    baked in as light and shade.
  - The model was fitted onto The Specialists' own M4A1 models, so the hands
    and every animation (reload, draw, the shots and the gunbash) are the
    game's.
  - None of the original file, nor its full-size textures, is included here.
