Mapping for H9's Xash: the limits, exactly
==========================================

H9XASH runs Xash3D FWGS, which loads maps far past what Valve's GoldSrc takes. H9's
compilers (export-map\sdhlt-goldsrc-net) hold a map to one engine's limits or the
other's, chosen on the command line. This file lists every limit a map meets, from the
compiler to the running game, each with its exact number and where it comes from.


1. Choosing the engine
----------------------

Every compiler takes the same switch, and all four must get the same one:

  -engine xash      (or -xash)   Xash3D FWGS's limits: the numbers in the "Xash" columns
  -engine goldsrc   (or nothing)  stock GoldSrc's: the "GoldSrc" columns, as before

The tools do not look at the map to decide. Each log starts with "Limits of GoldSrc"
or "Limits of Xash3D FWGS", so a log always says which one it held the map to.

The file comes out in the smallest layout that fits (-format bsp30|bsp30ext|bsp2 to
force one):

  bsp30      the stock version 30 file: 16-bit numbers everywhere. Every engine loads it.
  bsp30ext   version 30 with 32-bit clipnodes, past 32,767 of them. Xash only.
  BSP2       Xash's 32-bit layout, past any 16-bit count (vertexes, nodes, leaves,
             faces, marksurfaces). Xash only.

Steam's Half-Life, HLDS and SMAK's map readers (renders, views, compiled-map checks)
take version 30 files only.


2. What the compilers hold a map to
-----------------------------------

The arrays are sized for Xash; the limit enforced is the chosen engine's
(sdhlt common/bspfile.h, and the per-engine table in common/bspfile.cpp).

                          GoldSrc        Xash          Over Xash's limit, the engine
  brushes, in all         32,768         32,768        (compiler only: hlcsg stops)
  brush sides             196,608        196,608       (6 a brush; compiler only)
  entities, compiled      16,384         16,384        (see 4 for what runs)
  entity text             2 MB           2 MB          warns
  brush models            512            2,048         refuses the map
  planes                  32,768         131,072       warns
  vertexes                65,535         524,288       warns
  nodes                   32,767         262,144       refuses the map
  leaves                  32,760         131,072       refuses the map
    world leaves          8,192 (warns)  131,072
  faces                   65,535         262,144       refuses the map
    world faces           32,768         262,144
  texinfo                 32,767         32,767        refuses past 262,144
  clipnodes               32,767         524,288       warns
    in one hull of one    32,767         32,767        (bsp30ext only; BSP2 has none)
    model
  marksurfaces            65,535         524,288       warns
  edges                   256,000        1,048,576     warns
  surfedges               512,000        2,097,152     warns
  textures in the map     4,096          4,096         (renderer: 8,192 loaded at once)
  texture data            32 MB          32 MB         warns past 32 MB
  light data              48 MB          48 MB         warns past 32 MB
  vis data                8 MB           16 MB         warns
  lightmap pages          64             256           stops loading: "LM_AllocBlock: full"
                          A page is 128 x 128 luxels in a version 30 or bsp30ext map. Xash
                          makes it 1,024 x 1,024 in a BSP2 map (or with r_large_lightmaps 1),
                          so a BSP2 map has 64 times the room. The compiler's count assumes
                          128 x 128.
  hlbsp t-junction        0x40000        0x100000      (compiler only)
    edge points
  hlbsp t-junction edges  0x20000        0x80000       (compiler only)

Of these, only the brush count (32,768 in all, detail and brush entities included) is
the same ceiling for both engines. Every other limit in the Xash column is Xash's own,
read from its common/bspfile.h and its map loader's lump table
(engine/common/mod_bmodel.c).

The four limits that refuse a map outright are nodes, texinfo, faces and leaves (the
loader's CHECK_OVERFLOW lumps), plus brush models. Past any other limit it warns in
the console and loads the map anyway.

hlvis and hlrad have limits of their own:

  vis leaves (portal leafs)    32,760   both engines. hlvis works on the BSP's leaves
                                        grouped round detail brushes, so a map with
                                        40,000 leaves can have 4,000 vis leaves.
  portals on one vis leaf      256
  light patches                1,048,560 (65,535 x 16)
  light styles on one face     4
  light styles in the map      64 (switchable lights use 32 to 63)
  lightmap density             one luxel per 16 units of a face; hlbsp splits faces
                               at 240 units (-subdivide), keeping each under 16 luxels


3. How far a map can reach
--------------------------

  stock GoldSrc         +/-4,096   (the network's origin bits)
  H9 on Xash            +/-32,768

H9 widens the network to +/-32,768 in two places:
- ts\delta.lst gives every origin enough bits:
  - entities 19 bits in eighths;
  - players 21 bits in 32nds;
  - client data 23 bits in 128ths;
  - custom entities and events 19 bits in eighths.
- engine-h9-bigworld.patch (xash3d-h9-patches) writes coordinates as whole units,
  +/-32,768, whenever the world reaches past +/-4,096.

User messages from the game DLL keep eighths, as its client reads them.

hlcsg accepts geometry to +/-65,536 (-worldextent), so stay inside +/-32,768 yourself.
Its "origin outside +/-4096" warnings for entities are GoldSrc's, and harmless on H9.


4. What the running game holds
------------------------------

Xash3D FWGS's own limits (engine/common/protocol.h and common.h, client/sound.h,
ref/gl/gl_local.h, filesystem/gameinfo.c):

  edicts                       8,192 at most (13 bits). The mod's own number rules:
                               H9's liblist.gam and gameinfo.txt say 4,098; Xash's
                               default without one is 1,200. Every door, func_wall,
                               sprite, sound and player takes one. A light without a
                               targetname frees its edict as it spawns.
  entities sent per frame      2,048 (GoldSrc: 256)
  model precache               4,096 (GoldSrc: 512)
  sound precache               2,048 (GoldSrc: 512)
  generic files                1,024
  events                       1,024
  user messages                197
  light styles                 256 on the network; the compiler writes 64
  dynamic lights in a frame    32
  entity lights in a frame     128
  static entities              3,096
  decals                       512 sent, 4,096 drawn in a level
  static sound channels        256 (every looping ambient_generic takes one, near or
                               far); 64 dynamic channels besides
  leaves an entity touches     48 in a version 30 map, 24 in a 32-bit one. A brush
                               entity spread over more leaves can drop out of view:
                               keep doors and func_walls compact in a big map.
  players                      32


5. A worked example: New Byzantium
----------------------------------

h9_tsrp_newbyzantium (export-map\h9_tsrp_newbyzantium), compiled -engine xash, is a
BSP2 file. Its counts against the Xash column:

  brushes        29,846 of 32,768   (17,129 world, the rest detail and entities)
  entities       3,868 compiled; doors, signs, sprites and 240 sounds run
  leaves         46,737 of 131,072  (world 40,616)
  vis leaves     4,100 of 32,760, with 6,045 portals
  faces          128,464 of 262,144
  nodes          68,121 of 262,144
  clipnodes      171,493 of 524,288
  marksurfaces   203,995 of 524,288
  vertexes       191,717 of 524,288
  edges          328,893 of 1,048,576
  surfedges      601,370 of 2,097,152
  planes         33,415 of 131,072

The brush count is its real ceiling. The city's rooms are carved from rock tile by
tile, and a floor between stacked rooms is one detail slab, so that it stays under
32,768.


6. Where the numbers come from
------------------------------

  export-map\sdhlt-goldsrc-net\src\sdhlt\common\bspfile.h     the compilers' arrays
  export-map\sdhlt-goldsrc-net\src\sdhlt\common\bspfile.cpp   s_goldsrc and s_xash
  export-map\sdhlt-goldsrc-net\src\sdhlt\sdHLVIS\vis.h        vis leaves, portals
  export-map\sdhlt-goldsrc-net\src\sdhlt\sdHLRAD\qrad.h       patches, light styles
  xash3d-fwgs\common\bspfile.h                                Xash's map limits
  xash3d-fwgs\engine\common\mod_bmodel.c                      which of them refuse
  xash3d-fwgs\engine\common\protocol.h, common.h              network and edicts
  xash3d-fwgs\engine\client\sound.h                           sound channels
  xash3d-fwgs\ref\gl\gl_local.h                               lightmap pages
  H9XASH\ts\delta.lst, xash3d-h9-patches\patches\engine-h9-bigworld.patch
                                                              how far a map reaches

The changes that let the compilers take Xash's limits carry a "// 2CWLDYS" line in
the source, each beside what it changed.
