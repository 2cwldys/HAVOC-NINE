ASSAULT -- the capture-point mode: how it plays, every number you can change, how to make a map
================================================================================================

ts_assault.amxx on the ts_matchmode core. Maps named h9_as_<name>. Its cvars are in configs\ts_assault.cfg
(and the three presets' copies in H9XASH\presets), plus the core's pool cvars in ts_matchmode.cfg. This is
the reference for the mode; docs\GAME_MODES.txt says what each kind of map plays and how a map is set up in
general, and docs\CUSTOM_SPAWNPOINTS.txt part 5 is the spawn sets.

Not to be mixed up with the Assault CLASS, the first of the nine (the one with the M4A1): it has nothing to
do with the mode but the name. "Assault" below is the mode.


1. WHAT IT IS
-------------

Both sides fight over the capture points of the map (2 to 24 of them). A point is taken by standing in it,
nothing is carried, and nothing needs an uplink. A side's lives are ONE pool, called REINFORCEMENTS: a death
draws from it by class, the dead come back in respawn waves at the spawn points of a point their side holds,
and a side with nobody alive and nobody waiting loses. Holding every point wins at once. At the clock more
points win, then more reinforcements. By default the first round that ends is the whole match.


2. HOW IT PLAYS
---------------

The points. A point is a circle across the floor, tsm_as_radius wide (384 units unless the map says
otherwise; an uplink zone is 128), with a height window of 128 units up or down from the point's floor. It
has a letter (A is point 1, B point 2, up to X for 24), a ring on the floor in its holder's colour (grey
nobody, blue MSR, red Insurgents, amber while it is being taken) and a flag over it. Where circles overlap
the nearest centre wins.

Taking a point. Every living player in play on a side counts while he stands in it, bots included. With one
side in it and no living enemy there, that side takes it: one player takes a neutral point in tsm_as_time
(20) seconds, and each further player adds half again as fast (tsm_as_speedup 0.5) up to tsm_as_maxcap (6)
players:

     capturers    1     2     3     4     5     6 or more
     seconds     20.0  13.3  10.0   8.0   6.7   5.7

A point the other side holds is worn down to neutral first and then built up for the taker, so it takes twice
that. Both sides in it at once is CONTESTED: nothing moves. Nobody in it: it keeps what it had, with no decay.
Half a capture stays when the taker leaves.

What a capture gives. tsm_score_zone (5) scoreboard points to every in-play player of the capturing side who
is standing in the circle at that moment; tsm_as_capture_reinforcements (5) added to the side's pool; the
point's spawn set opens for that side; and with tsm_as_capture_wave 1 (the default) every dead player of the
side waiting for a wave comes back at once. Losing a point costs a side nothing; it only closes the point's
spawn set. Holding EVERY point wins the round on the spot.

Reinforcements. Each side starts a round with tsm_pool_base (16) plus tsm_pool_perplayer (4) for every player
on the side, bots included while tsm_bots is above 0, as the round goes live: 5 a side 36, 8 a side 48, 16 a
side 80, 32 a side 144. A death in a live round draws its class's cost from the pool (tsm_class_cost): the
Officer 2, the Mech 3, every other class 1, and the dead player waits for the next wave. A death when the pool
is at 0 is FINAL: he is out for the rest of the round; reinforcements won later do not bring him back, they
only pay for later deaths. A pool smaller than the cost is emptied by it and that player still waits for a
wave. The per-class lives (tsm_class_lives) are not used. Needs tsm_respawns 1: at 0 every death is final.

Waves. Each side has its own clock of tsm_as_wave (30) seconds, started as the round goes live. A timed wave
returns the players who have been dead at least tsm_respawn_seconds (5); a capture wave, the Officer's
.reinforce and amx_tsm_wave return everyone waiting. TS's own death timer still applies, and a revived player
gets the full class kit and health, the Officer his kung fu again; an Officer's fall is not announced to both
sides ("officer has perished", tsm_officerslain_sound) in this mode: his death costs reinforcements, not the Officer.

Where a wave lands. Point N's SPAWN SET is the spawn points of tier N in the map's spawn file. A set is open
to the side that holds point N, and shut when the point is lost, and (tsm_as_spawn_under_attack 0) while an
enemy is taking it; a contested point keeps its set as it was. A wave lands on ONE set: of the sets that are
open to the side and have a point for it, the FRONT one, whose points lie nearest the middle of the enemy's
base (the lowest number on a tie). A side that holds nothing lands at its own base (tier 0). Round start and a
player who is alive are never moved. A player placed more than once during a revive stays on the same set.

Winning. In order: a side holds every point (at once, after a capture); a side has nobody alive and nobody
waiting (after tsm_roundend_delay, 4 seconds; both at once is a draw); at the clock (tsm_roundtime: the
preset's 300, the shipped maps' 720, 720 and 900) the side with more points wins, then the one with more
reinforcements LEFT, and equal is a draw. Partial captures count for nothing. There is no sudden death and no
bleed: only deaths and captures change a pool.

One round, one match. h9_assault_rounds 0 (the default): the first round that ends is the whole match, its
winner the match's, a draw a drawn match; the match bar and the chat say MATCH; at the end the bar says
"<SIDE> WINS", then "MATCH OVER" 3 seconds later, and the server is back in warmup 5 seconds after that.
h9_assault_rounds 1 plays rounds like the other modes: best of tsm_rounds (first to rounds/2 + 1; 9 is first
to 5); every round starts with every point neutral and every set shut. The cvar is read within a second, so it
can be changed during a match (a match whose rounds were already counted is then decided by that tally).

Bots fight but do not play the objective: nothing tells them where the points are yet. A bot that happens to
stand in a circle still counts as a capturer or a contester.


3. THE NUMBERS YOU CAN CHANGE
-----------------------------

Where to set them: configs\ts_assault.cfg and ts_matchmode.cfg for the server, the preset files in
H9XASH\presets for a preset, or the map's own ts\mapconfigs\<map>.cfg for one map. A cvar typed at the server
console works too. "Read" says when a change counts.

3a. REINFORCEMENTS -- how many a side has

     tsm_pool_base             16   the flat part of a side's starting pool
     tsm_pool_perplayer         4   added for every player on the side when the round goes live
     tsm_class_cost       1,1,1,1,1,1,2,1,3
                                    what a death of each class draws, in the order of the tsm_classN
                                    lines (7th, the Officer, 2; 9th, the Mech, 3); below 1 is read as 1
     tsm_as_capture_reinforcements 5  added to a side's pool when it takes a point (0: none)
     tsm_respawns               1   must be 1: at 0 there are no waves and every death is final

The pool is base + perplayer x players, at most 4095, set once as the round goes live: a change counts from
the NEXT round going live, never in the middle of one. tsm_class_cost is read when classes are set up at
each round start. To give a side more to fight with, raise tsm_pool_base (a flat amount) or tsm_pool_perplayer
(a lot more on a full server); to make the big classes cheaper or dearer, change tsm_class_cost; to make
points worth fighting for, raise tsm_as_capture_reinforcements. No console command edits a pool during a
round (the natives tsm_pool_set and tsm_pool_add exist for plugins).

3b. THE POINTS AND THEIR CAPTURE

     tsm_assault_mode           1   the mode on (0: an h9_as_ map plays as plain team elimination); read when
                                    the map is claimed, 2 seconds after it loads
     tsm_as_radius            384   reach of a point that brings none of its own (minimum 32); a brush point
                                    is as wide as its box. Used when the point is made
     tsm_as_time               20   seconds for one player to take a neutral point (live)
     tsm_as_speedup           0.5   extra speed for each further capturer (live)
     tsm_as_maxcap              6   most capturers of a side that count (live)
     tsm_as_capture_wave        1   1: the taker's waiting dead return at once (live)
     tsm_as_spawn_under_attack  0   0: a point's spawn set is shut while an enemy is taking it; 1: it stays
                                    open (live)

3c. ROUNDS, WAVES AND THE CLOCK

     h9_assault_rounds          0   0: one round is the match; 1: rounds, best of tsm_rounds
     tsm_as_wave               30   seconds between both sides' waves (the next round start; the core's
                                    tsm_respawn_wave is not used while this is above 0)
     tsm_respawn_seconds      5.0   a timed wave takes only those dead at least this long
     tsm_reinforce_cooldown   120   seconds before an Officer may call .reinforce again
     tsm_roundtime            300   the round clock (the shipped maps' mapconfigs set 720, 720, 900); 0: none
     tsm_rounds                 9   best-of, only with h9_assault_rounds 1
     tsm_roundend_delay       4.0   seconds a side with nobody left is held before the round ends
     tsm_freezetime             6   seconds frozen before the round goes live (the preset's; 10 is the default)
     tsm_score_zone             5   scoreboard points to each capturer in the circle at a capture
     tsm_gamedesc_assault  Assault  the game's name in the server browser and on the Discord card
     tsm_class_limits   0,4,2,2,4,2,1,4,1
                                    most of each class a side may have (the Officer 1, the Mech 1), a
                                    little more on a bigger server (tsm_scale_players)
     tsm_bots                  10   bots the server keeps (the preset's); any bot in a match means it is
                                    not rated
     tsm_verbosity              0   0 silences every [MATCH] chat line (the mode's too); 1 shows them

Chat is silent as shipped. At tsm_verbosity 0 the points, the bars, the news banners and the match bar still
tell everything an H9 game needs; a game that draws no H9 marks learns of a point changing hands from the
point's own marker and two sounds only.


4. COMMANDS
-----------

Players (chat):
     .reinforce          the Officer's call: returns EVERY waiting teammate at once, bots included, resets
                         his side's wave clock, once per tsm_reinforce_cooldown. It costs no reinforcements
                         (they were paid at death) and brings back nobody whose death was final. Nothing is
                         spent when nobody is waiting.
     .class .ready .unready .forfeit .status    the ordinary match commands. The class menu shows each
                         class's cost where other modes show its lives; a class is picked once, in the freeze.

Admins (ADMIN_CFG), the capture points:
     amx_tsm_assault                  the mode's state: build, whether it is active, each side's pool, the
                                      points and who holds them
     amx_tsm_as_list                  every point: number, letter, x y z, radius, holder, where it came from
     amx_tsm_as_add [n] [radius]      a point at your feet (the lowest free number, tsm_as_radius); two
                                      points make a map an Assault map on the spot
     amx_tsm_as_del <n>               remove a point (one the map itself defines returns on the next load)
     amx_tsm_as_radius <n> <radius>   a point's reach, 32 or more
     amx_tsm_as_give <n> <0|1|2>      hand a point to a side, or to nobody, to test: opens or shuts its
                                      spawn set and updates the marks, pays nothing and never ends the round
     amx_tsm_as_save                  write configs\tsm_assault_<map>.cfg (replaces the file: hand comments
                                      such as the points' names are lost; points the map defines are not written)
     tsm_as_zone <n> <x> <y> <z> [radius]   the server command the file is made of

Admins, the spawn sets and testing (ts_matchmode):
     amx_tsm_spawn_add <1|2> [tier]   a spawn point where you stand; tier 0 is a base, tier N the spawn set of
                                      point N (up to 32); then amx_tsm_spawn_save. amx_tsm_spawn_list, _del,
                                      _clear, _tp
     amx_tsm_debug                    each side's pool, the cvars, and the spawn sets open and the tier waves land at
     amx_tsm_wave [1|2]               force a wave now
     amx_tsm_simelim [player]         eliminate a player as if killed (draws the pool)
     amx_tsm_simwin <1|2>             award the round

A tool walks a BSP and proposes the points and the spawn sets: node tools\assault-spawns\cli.mjs <map>
--suggest <n> --plan <folder> (docs\ai_context\ASSAULT_SPAWNS.txt).


5. WHAT PLAYERS SEE
-------------------

Through the walls: every point on every screen, living, dead or spectating. An H9 game draws a badge with the
point's letter in its holder's colour (blue MSR, red Insurgents, grey nobody; the frame blinks yellow while
contested), a bar while a side is taking it, the badge pinned to the screen's edge when the point is out of
view, and the same badges in a row under the top notices (client cvars cl_h9_marks, cl_h9_marks_strip;
docs\H9_VGUI.txt). Any other game gets TS's own objective marker: the letter, the holder, and TAKING 45% or
CONTESTED.

On the floor: the ring and the flag (the Uplink's sprites). Standing in a point: "POINT A -- MSR TAKING IT" in
the taker's colour with a bar (or a hud line without H9 VGUI), or "POINT A IS CONTESTED". A capture rings a
bell map-wide and a loss a click. H9 games get a 3-second banner for each change ("MSR took point A -- 2 of 5
held"); the chat carries the same at tsm_verbosity 1.

The match bar: "MATCH -- 11:49 -- POINTS 2 - 1  REINFORCEMENTS 31 - 28" over "MSR -- INS" (ROUND n and the
tally with h9_assault_rounds 1). The scoreboard's lives column becomes REINFORCEMENTS on each team's row, with
what is left out of what it started with. A dead player reads "RESPAWN WAVE IN 12s -- 30 reinforcements left";
a living Officer reads "WAVE IN 12s -- REINFORCE READY" and, while teammates wait, a list at the right of the
screen (a panel on an H9 game, a hud list on any other): WAITING FOR A WAVE (3), their names, and how many more.


6. MAKING AN ASSAULT MAP
------------------------

1. Name it h9_as_<name>. A copy of an Uplink map keeps that map's zones: put tsm_uplink_mode 0 in its mapconfig.
   No tsm_obj_ siege objectives on it.
2. The points: entities (info_target, or a brush for its footprint) with the targetname tsm_as_1 to tsm_as_24, or
   lines in configs\tsm_assault_<map>.cfg, "tsm_as_zone <n> <x> <y> <z> [radius]" (the file wins over an
   entity of the same number). Stand where a point should be and amx_tsm_as_add, then amx_tsm_as_save.
3. The spawns: configs\tsm_spawns_<map>.cfg, with for EACH side its base (tier 0, enough for the whole team)
   and, for every point N that side may hold, a set of tier N: near the point but behind it from the enemy's
   way in and outside the circle, about 8 points 64 units apart (docs\CUSTOM_SPAWNPOINTS.txt part 5).
4. A mapconfig, ts\mapconfigs\<map>.cfg: tsm_respawns 1 and tsm_roundtime (720 is a fair start).
5. The sprites (tsm_zone_ring_*.spr, tsm_zone_flag.spr) are optional: without them there is no ring, flag or
   sound, and the mode still runs. rcbot waypoints for the bots. Mapconfigs run on H9XASH only, not on a
   Steam hlds server.
6. tools\make_assault_maps.py makes the shipped maps from maps that already play (copies of the bsp, the
   waypoints and the picture; writes the config files only when they are missing).

Limits: 24 points, 32 marks, spawn tiers 1 to 32, 256 spawn points a side, pools 0 to 4095, radius 32 up.


7. RANKING, THE CARD, THE SITE
------------------------------

The match is on the MATCH ladder (docs\H9_COMPETITIVE.txt). The one round is rated as the round it is: h9_compete
says the match was a single round (oneRound) and havoc9.org then asks one round and the five minutes, not
three rounds. A drawn match and a match with a bot in it rate nobody (the Assault preset keeps 10 bots; turn
tsm_bots off for a ranked server). The server must run the official plugins, or it reads "modified".

The server publishes tsm_pres_mode "assault" and tsm_pres_prog "2/1/5" (side 1's points, side 2's, all of
them). The Discord card reads "Assault - points: MSR 2, Insurgents 1 of 5" (a game older than 2026-11-05 shows
Deathmatch); the in-game server browser's Mode column and havoc9.org read "Assault".

9. THINGS TO KNOW
-----------------

 * The preset's tsm_roundtime is 300 and the maps' mapconfigs say 720 and 900: the map's wins.
 * tsm_sudden_death does nothing here: the mode has no sudden death.
 * tsm_assault_mode 0 after the map was claimed does not release it.
 * A point that came from a map entity and was deleted or resized in the editor returns on the next load.
 * The pool is 0 from the start of a round's freeze until it goes live; a bar drawn then shows 0.
 * The points keep their holders through the end-of-match warmup until the next round starts.
