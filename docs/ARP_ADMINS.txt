===============================================================================
 GIVING APOLLO RP ADMIN TO A PLAYER (BY HIS XASH PLAYER ID OR HIS HAVOC9.ORG ACCOUNT)
===============================================================================

 Apollo RP (ARP) has an idea of an admin of its own, apart from AMX Mod X's. This is how to give it to one person: to the player who
 is in the game now, to one who is not, or by the havoc9.org account he plays under. For the owner of a roleplay server; nothing here
 is secret, and nothing secret may be put in it.

 CONTENTS
    1. What an ARP admin is
    2. The first one: you
    3. A player who is in the game now
    4. A player who is not (his xash player id, or his account)
    5. The Administrator job, on H9's own maps
    6. Taking it away
    7. Checking it, and when it does not work
    8. Giving yourself money, items and a job (testing)

 1. WHAT AN ARP ADMIN IS
 -----------------------
 ARP's rights are 26 letters, a to z. A character holds two sets of them, and has both: its own, which arp_setaccess sets and which is
 saved with the character (the access column of the table arp_users), and its job's (the access column of arp_jobs, or of the map's own
 jobs table). The letter that makes an ARP admin is the one in arp_admin_access, in configs\arp\arp.cfg: z as shipped. A character
 that holds it may run every command marked (ADMIN) in ARP and the arp_config menu.
   * The server's console, rcon, and the player of a listen server (the game started from the menu is player 1) need no letter: they
     are always let in.
   * Never empty arp_admin_access. With no letter named there, ARP lets every player run its admin commands.
   * AMX Mod X's own admins (configs\users.ini) are not ARP admins, and ARP admins are not AMX Mod X's. The two exceptions are
     arp_addjob and arp_deletejob, which test both, and which are for a player in the game: the server's console is refused with
     an error in the AMX log. Everything else in this guide works from the server console.

 2. THE FIRST ONE: YOU
 ---------------------
 A dedicated server: from its console, or from your game over rcon (rcon_password <password>, then "rcon <command>"; an rcon command is
 the server console's). A listen server: from the console of your own game. Join the server, and in the server's console:
     status
 lists each player with his name, his userid (a small number, good for this visit only) and his uniqueid, his id on this server:
 "ID_" and 32 letters and digits (section 4). A line looks like this; the userid is the number right after the quoted name (3 here),
 not the "# 1" at the far left, which is only his slot:
     #      name userid uniqueid frag time ping loss adr
     # 1 "Ann" 3 ID_0123456789abcdef0123456789abcdef   0 02:10   35    0 192.168.1.5:27005
 Then:
     arp_setaccess #<your userid> z
 You are an ARP admin from that moment, and the letter is saved with your character: after 30 seconds (arp_save_interval) or when you
 leave. A listen server's player 1 is let in without it, but the letter keeps the rights if the same character joins a dedicated one.

 3. A PLAYER WHO IS IN THE GAME NOW
 ----------------------------------
 From the server's console, or from the console of any ARP admin:
     arp_setaccess "<his name>" z          (a part of the name will do, if it is only his)
     arp_setaccess #<his userid> z         (the number "status" shows)
 What it sets is the whole of his own set of letters, not one to add: "arp_setaccess #5 bz" gives b as well as z, and "arp_setaccess #5
 z" takes a b that he had. His job's letters are not touched. It takes effect at once. AMX Mod X refuses the command on a player whose
 AMX Mod X flags include immunity (a), unless he runs it himself.

 4. A PLAYER WHO IS NOT (HIS XASH PLAYER ID, OR HIS ACCOUNT)
 ------------------------------------------------------------
 Two ids name a player for good; the userid that "status" shows is for one visit only:
   * His xash player id on this server: the "ID_" and 32 characters H9XASH makes for him, MD5 of his own random id and this server's
     IPv4 address (docs\CUSTOM_XASH_USERID.txt). It is the key of his character in the database, the authid column of arp_users, and
     it changes when the server's address does. "status" prints it as the uniqueid while he is in the game.
   * His havoc9.org account number, accounts.id: the number in front of his name in the account list (the admin panel's ACCOUNTS tab,
     or the list command of docs\H9WEB_ADMIN.txt, section 4). It never changes. ARP knows it only for a player whose game is linked to
     the account and proved it to the server (h9_presence; apollo-rp\ARP_IDS.txt): the table arp_ids lists the xash
     ids each account has played under here, and the first of them is the key of the account's character, whatever address the
     server has now.
 The grant is a row of arp_users, and the server's console does it with arp_query, which runs one SQL statement and prints nothing.
 Do it for a character that is NOT in the game: a character in the game is saved over it within 30 seconds. With his xash id:
     arp_query UPDATE arp_users SET access='z' WHERE authid='ID_0123456789abcdef0123456789abcdef'
 With his account number, say 417, first the key that account's character is under (the server stopped, or on a copy: arp_query shows no
 rows; data\sqlite3\arp.sq3 opens in any SQLite tool, python's sqlite3 module will do):
     SELECT authid FROM arp_ids WHERE acct=417 ORDER BY id LIMIT 1
 and then the same UPDATE with that authid. No row means he has not played here with his account proved: he joins once (the game
 links his account in the main menu, "Link your Account"), the HUD says his account is loading for a moment, and then the account's
 character is the one to give it to, in the game, as in section 3. A character with no row in arp_users yet gets one at his first
 join, and the UPDATE of an id with no row changes nothing: make him join once, then give it.
 Back up data\sqlite3\arp.sq3 first (copy the file with the server stopped).

 5. THE ADMINISTRATOR JOB, ON H9'S OWN MAPS
 ------------------------------------------
 The jobs of h9_tsrp_hive and h9_tsrp_newbyzantium include one named Administrator, with the letter z and no salary
 (docs\ARP_CONTENT.txt, section 3). An admin gives it as any job:
     arp_setjob #<userid> "Administrator"
 He is an ARP admin for as long as he holds it, and no rank in the roleplay has z, so nobody else can offer it. It is the map's: a
 map whose jobs table has no such job answers "Could not find a job id matching what you said", and the letter in section 3 is the
 way there. A job of any map can carry z: arp_addjob "<name>" <salary> z, by a player in the game with AMX Mod X rights (section 1).

 6. TAKING IT AWAY
 -----------------
     arp_setaccess #<userid> ""            (no letters of his own; his job's stay)
     arp_setjob #<userid> "Unemployed"     (when it came with the Administrator job)
 For a character not in the game, UPDATE arp_users SET access='' WHERE authid='<his id>' as in section 4. Both are saved with the
 character. Anyone else who has a letter z, in his job or his own, is an admin: look at the jobs table (arp_query is blind; read it
 with an SQLite tool) and at the access column of arp_users.

 7. CHECKING IT, AND WHEN IT DOES NOT WORK
 -----------------------------------------
 He types arp_hudstate in his console: an admin gets a list of the players and the HUD each is sent, anyone else a refusal (no
 access). Every (ADMIN) command is the same test. If it fails:
   * The letter is not the one arp_admin_access names: arp_admin_access in the server's console prints it.
   * The grant was made to the wrong character: the one in the game is the one under "status", and a player whose havoc9.org account
     is proved while another is not may be a different character from the one he had yesterday (apollo-rp\ARP_IDS.txt).
   * It was written to a character that was in the game, and saved over: make it in the game (section 3), not with arp_query.
   * ARP is not running on this map: ARP stands down on a map that is not a roleplay one (apollo-rp\ORIGIN.txt, 1), and its
     commands are not there. The logs in addons\amxmodx\logs record the admin commands that were run ("Cmd: ..." lines) and the
     errors a command raised.


 8. GIVING YOURSELF MONEY, ITEMS AND A JOB (TESTING)
 ---------------------------------------------------
 An ARP admin (section 1) can give himself what he needs to try something, from the server's console or from his own console. <you> is your name or
 #<your userid> (the number after your name in "status", section 2).
   Money      arp_createmoney "<you>" 5000        adds to your wallet        arp_setmoney "<you>" 5000        sets it
              arp_createbank "<you>" 20000        adds to your bank          arp_setbank "<you>" 20000        sets it
   Items      arp_createitems "<you>" "Laser Rifle" 1        arp_createitems "<you>" "Cuffs" 2        arp_createitems "<you>" "Rope" 1
              arp_removeitems "<you>" "Rope" 1               arp_setitems "<you>" "Soda" 5
              The item is a number or a part of its name; arp_itemlist prints them all. A part that fits two items is refused ("Bug" is also
              "Bug Scanner"): say more of the name, or give the number.
   A job      arp_setjob "<you>" "Cadet"          police rights (/cuff, /fine, jail) at once;  "Administrator" on H9's maps; arp_joblist lists jobs
   Rights     arp_setaccess "<you>" z             your own rights (section 3); arp_setaccess "<you>" ""  takes them away
 A worked example, to try cuffing and searching: two players are in the game. The first runs arp_setjob "<first>" "Cadet" and arp_createmoney
 "<second>" 500. The first looks at the second, within 200 units, and says /cuff: the second is cuffed (a red glow, a handcuff symbol over his head).
 The first presses the use key (E) on him: the search window opens with the second's items and his wallet, and TAKE 10, TAKE 100 and TAKE ALL take
 money. To try the Cuffs item with no police job: arp_createitems "<civilian>" "Cuffs" 1, and he uses it (inventory window, or /cuff) on another
 civilian; a cop cannot be cuffed.
 docs\ARP_COMMANDS.txt lists every command.
