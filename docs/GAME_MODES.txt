GAME MODES -- what each H9 map plays, and how to set a map up for each
=====================================================================

ts_matchmode is the core: rounds, sides, lives and respawn waves, classes, the HUD. Three
mode plugins sit on top of it, each on the maps that are its own: ts_uplink.amxx (Capture
the Uplink), ts_survival.amxx (Wave Survival) and ts_mechwarrior.amxx (Mech Warrior). They
talk to the core through a small API (include\tsm_api.inc) and do nothing without it.

Everything a mapper needs is here: what a map's NAME does, the entities and configs each
mode reads, and the in-game commands that write those configs for you. Nothing is needed
beyond the map editor and the game itself.


1. WHICH GAME A MAP PLAYS
-------------------------
The map's name decides, as it loads. TS picks its gamerules (teamplay or deathmatch) while
the world spawns; ts_matchmode sets mp_teamplay just before that, so the right rules are in
place from the first second, on a Steam hlds server and on H9XASH alike.

  Name                   Plays                                       Rules
  h9_te_<name>           Team Elimination (the match mode)           teamplay
  h9_siege_<name>        Siege (the match mode with objectives)      teamplay
  h9_up_<name>           Capture the Uplink                          teamplay
  h9_ws_<name>           Wave Survival                               teamplay
  h9_mw_<name>           Mech Warrior                                teamplay
  any other h9_ or       Team Elimination, or Siege when the map     teamplay
  tsm_ name              has objectives
  h9_dm_<name>           TS's own deathmatch, as the game ships      deathmatch
  h9_tdm_<name>          TS's own team deathmatch                    teamplay
  h9_ctb_<name>          TS's own Capture the Briefcase              teamplay
  h9_ctf_<name>          TS's own Capture the Flag                   teamplay
  ts_<name>, and any     the game as it ships: TS's deathmatch       deathmatch
  other name
  roleplay names         the game as it ships, whatever else the     deathmatch
                         name says: rp_ or tsrp_ at the start, or
                         _rp_ or _tsrp_ anywhere (h9_tsrp_hive)

"As the game ships" means the match mode stays out entirely: no warmup or ready-up, no
side or class menus, no bots of its own (tsm_bots is 0 there), TS's own cash (usecash 0)
and powerups, and the server browser shows TS's own game name. A map's own worldspawn
"gamemode" key (1 deathmatch, 2 teamplay, 3 last man standing, 4 The One) still wins over
the rules column, as it does in the stock game.

Name a map for what it plays: the part after h9_ is also what the Discord card and the
server list call the mode.

THE MAP'S OWN MAPCONFIG HAS THE LAST WORD (section 4). Two lines in it are read as the map
loads, ahead of the name: "tsm_enabled 1" makes any map a match-mode map on teamplay rules,
"tsm_enabled 0" makes even an h9_ map play as the game ships, and "mp_teamplay 0" or "1"
picks the rules alone. A stock map can be played as a match without renaming it:

    // ts\mapconfigs\ts_bikini.cfg
    tsm_enabled 1

The server log says what each map got: "[TSM] <map>: an H9 map, the match mode plays, on
teamplay rules" or "not an H9 map, the game as it ships, on deathmatch rules".


2. THE MODES
------------

TEAM ELIMINATION  (ts_matchmode.amxx, configs\ts_matchmode.cfg)
  Two sides, MSR and the Insurgents, in elimination rounds: wipe the other side out. Each
  player picks a class at the freeze; every class has lives (tsm_class_lives), and the
  fallen come back with their side's respawn wave (every tsm_respawn_wave, 60, seconds)
  while their lives last. The last minute is sudden death (tsm_sudden_death). A match is
  best of tsm_rounds (9). Everyone readies up with .ready in chat; COMMANDS.txt has every
  command and cvar.

SIEGE  (ts_matchmode.amxx)
  Team elimination with objectives: the attacking side (tsm_obj_team) plants charges on the
  map's objectives in order, and the last one falling wins the round. Every fall but the
  last gives lives and time back (tsm_siege_rewardlives, tsm_siege_extratime). A map is a
  siege map because it has objectives, whatever its name; no mode may claim one.

CAPTURE THE UPLINK  (ts_uplink.amxx, configs\ts_uplink.cfg)
  Both sides fight over one carried objective, the uplink.
  The uplink   appears each round at the map's uplink point. Walk over it to take it; the
               carrier wears it on their back, keeps every weapon, and is marked to BOTH
               sides. It drops where the carrier falls, and goes home after
               tsm_uplink_return (20) seconds untouched. "dropuplink" in the console (bind
               it) or .drop in chat lets it go.
  The zones    up to nine. Only the carrier takes a zone: tsm_cap_time (15) seconds inside
               it with no living enemy in it. Leaving, dropping, dying or a contest starts it
               over. Each zone shows a ring on the floor in its holder's colour (grey, MSR
               blue, Insurgent red; amber while being taken) and a flag over it.
  Lives        a taken zone gives lives back by tsm_uplink_rewardlives: 1 the taking side,
               2 both, 0 none. Lives are SET to the class's count, never added, and the
               fallen come back AT ONCE, not with the next wave. Needs tsm_respawns 1.
  Waves        on an uplink map tsm_uplink_wave (30), not tsm_respawn_wave, sets both sides'
               respawn waves; .reinforce resets to it too.
  Winning      holding every zone wins at once. A side wiped out (no lives, nobody waiting)
               loses at once, whatever it held. When the clock runs out the side holding
               more zones wins; equal is a draw. There is no sudden death.
  Bots         fight, but do not yet carry the uplink or take zones.
  Switch       tsm_uplink_mode 1 (0 turns it off everywhere). Browser name:
               tsm_gamedesc_uplink "Capture the Uplink".
  Admin        amx_tsm_uplink (the state), amx_tsm_cap_give <n> <0|1|2> (hand a zone over,
               to test).

WAVE SURVIVAL  (ts_survival.amxx, configs\ts_survival.cfg)
  Side 1 are the survivors; side 2 is a wave of bots that never runs out. A person picking
  side 2 is made a spectator.
  Waves        a wave is over only when its last mutant is dead. Then a breather of
               tsm_ws_wave (30) seconds, counted down on screen, and the next wave comes all
               at once: the count goes up, xom_laugh plays for everyone, every fallen
               survivor comes back, every survivor's lives are set to their class's count,
               and each wave is a new round for the once-a-round abilities: the stim, .ammo,
               the class abilities and the Mech's rockets are all ready again. No round
               clock, no sudden death; the HUD shows WAVE n.
  The run      ends when side 1 is wiped out: chat says how far they got (and the best on
               this map), the match is over, and it is back to warmup.
  It grows     health +tsm_ws_hp_per_wave (5) percent per wave; guns by tsm_ws_gun_odds
               (40% at first, 60% from wave 16, all from wave 25); the kung fu powerup after
               wave tsm_ws_kungfu_after (30).
  A stall      after tsm_ws_stall (120) seconds with three or fewer mutants left and none
               falling, they are taken off the board and the wave counts as cleared.
  Switch       tsm_mutants_mode 1. Browser name: tsm_gamedesc_survival "Wave Survival".
  Admin        amx_tsm_ws (the state), amx_tsm_ws_wave <n> (jump the count, to test).

MECH WARRIOR  (ts_mechwarrior.amxx, configs\ts_mechwarrior.cfg)
  The match as in team elimination -- rounds, respawn waves, sudden death -- with the
  classes cut down to two: one Officer a side on two lives, and as many Mechs as a side
  likes on three lives each. Every other class is gone from the class menu, and every bot
  plays a Mech.
  Classes      found by their perks in ts_matchmode.cfg's tsm_classN lines (the Officer's,
               perk 4, and the Mech's, perk 7), so reordering the classes does not break it.
               tsm_mw_officers (1, 0 for no Officer), tsm_mw_officer_lives (2), tsm_mw_mechs
               (0, no limit) and tsm_mw_mech_lives (3); a change counts from the next round.
               The lives need tsm_respawns 1.
  Switch       tsm_mechwarrior_mode 1. Browser name: tsm_gamedesc_mechwarrior "Mech
               Warrior".
  Admin        amx_tsm_mw (the state).


3. SETTING UP A MAP
-------------------
Build and compile the map as for any TS map, then:

  1. NAME IT for its mode (section 1): h9_te_, h9_siege_, h9_up_, h9_ws_ or h9_mw_.

  2. SPAWNS. Place info_player_deathmatch points in the map as for any TS map, then give
     each side its own, at least as many as players it will have (16 a side is a
     comfortable number): stand on each spot in the game, facing the way players should
     face, and use the commands, which write configs\tsm_spawns_<map>.cfg:
         amx_tsm_spawn_add 1 [tier]  (or 2) a point for that side where you stand
         amx_tsm_spawn_list          what is placed
         amx_tsm_spawn_tp <1|2> <n>  go to one, to check it
         amx_tsm_spawn_del <1|2> <n> take one away
         amx_tsm_spawn_save          write the file
     Without that file the match mode splits the map's deathmatch points into two groups
     by where they stand, which is only a guess. Leave 64 units between points: a player is
     never placed on top of somebody standing there. docs\CUSTOM_SPAWNPOINTS.txt has the
     rest (forward tiers for siege maps, pruning).

  3. WHAT THE MODE NEEDS
     Team Elimination  nothing more.
     Siege             the objectives: brush entities named tsm_obj_1 .. tsm_obj_9, in the
                       order they fall, and optionally tsm_obj_N_reset relays and
                       Engineer-only doors named tsm_eng_door. MAP_ENTITIES.txt is the full
                       account; amx_tsm_obj shows what the map has.
     Capture the       the zones and the uplink's point. In the map: any entity named
     Uplink            tsm_cap_1 .. tsm_cap_9 (an info_target's origin is a zone's middle,
                       tsm_cap_radius, 128, units round it; a brush entity's box is its
                       footprint) and an info_target named tsm_uplink. Or in the game, then
                       saved to configs\tsm_uplink_<map>.cfg:
                           amx_tsm_cap_add [n] [radius]   a zone where you stand
                           amx_tsm_cap_del <n>            remove one
                           amx_tsm_cap_list               the zones and who holds them
                           amx_tsm_uplink_set             the uplink's point where you stand
                           amx_tsm_uplink_save            write the file
                       Put the zones where both sides have to fight for them, and the
                       uplink's point between the two sides' starts, the same distance
                       from each.
     Wave Survival     the name alone. Spawns: side 1 (the survivors) somewhere they can
                       hold; side 2 (the mutants) where waves should come from, far enough
                       away that a wave arrives rather than appears. How many mutants a wave
                       has is tsm_bots, so set it in the mapconfig.
     Mech Warrior      the name alone. Mechs are slow, so give the round time
                       (tsm_roundtime) in the mapconfig.

  4. A MAPCONFIG (section 4) for anything this map plays differently: respawns, round time,
     how many bots.

  5. BOTS need waypoints to find their way: rcbot\waypoints\ts\<map>.rcw. Make them with
     rcbot's own waypoint editor in the game (its documentation is in the rcbot folder).
     Without them bots stand around.

  6. CONTENT a player may not have -- a sky, sounds, models, sprites, a wad -- goes in a
     <map>.res file beside the .bsp, one path per line relative to ts\ (gfx/env/<sky>rt.tga
     and so on), so the server sends it with the map. Textures can instead be put inside the
     .bsp by the compiler (-wadinclude).

  7. TEST IT: load the map, check the server log's line for which game it plays, and use
     amx_tsm_debug (the core), amx_tsm_obj (siege), amx_tsm_uplink (uplink), amx_tsm_ws
     (survival) or amx_tsm_mw (Mech Warrior) to see the mode's state. tsm_test_solo 1 lets a
     match run with only one side filled.

  8. THE DISCORD CARD shows a picture of the map when the HAVOC 9 Discord application has an
     art asset named after the map (docs\RICH_PRESENCE.txt).


4. PER-MAP CONFIGS
------------------
  ts\mapconfigs\<map>.cfg   any console commands and cvars, run by the H9XASH engine once the
                            map and its plugins are up (h9_autoconfig 1), so they override the
                            plugins' own configs for that map alone; the next map's configs
                            set everything back. Its tsm_enabled and mp_teamplay lines are also
                            read as the map loads (section 1). A Steam hlds server does not run
                            these files.
  configs\tsm_spawns_<map>.cfg    the spawn points (amx_tsm_spawn_save writes it).
  configs\tsm_uplink_<map>.cfg    the uplink's zones and point (amx_tsm_uplink_save):
                                  tsm_cap <n> <x> <y> <z> [radius] and tsm_uplink_at <x> <y> <z>.
  H9XASH\presets            h9_presets.bat swaps whole profiles: the .Siege copies of
                            ts_uplink.cfg, ts_survival.cfg and ts_mechwarrior.cfg have their mode
                            on, the .TeamElimination copies have it off (AMX_PRESETS.txt).

  A mapconfig, for example, ts\mapconfigs\h9_up_drainage.cfg:

      echo "[h9_autoconfig] h9_up_drainage.cfg applied"
      tsm_respawns 1
      tsm_roundtime 420


5. THE DRAINAGE MAPS  (export-map\h9_drainage)
----------------------------------------------
h9_up_drainage, h9_ws_drainage and h9_mw_drainage are one place: the city's storm-drain
works at night, a canal 704 deep with a basin at each end, two box culverts with open decks
over them, a pumping station in the middle, service galleries inside the walls, a filter
room and a generator room. Rain falls from high over the city's toxic storm sky, dirty haze
lies along the floors, and litter is everywhere. The sky gives little light; the lamps give
the rest, their lit faces shining. Over each basin the city's elevated line crosses on a
steel bridge between two tunnels, and every two minutes or so a train comes through, loud,
the ground shaking under everyone round the basin.

  h9_up_drainage   MSR on the north basin's platform, the Insurgents on the south's (16
                   each). Zones: 1 the north culvert, 2 the filter room, 3 the south culvert,
                   4 the generator room; the uplink in the station. Mapconfig:
                   tsm_respawns 1, tsm_roundtime 420.
  h9_ws_drainage   16 survivors in the station; mutants from both platforms and the
                   galleries' far ends. Mapconfig: tsm_respawns 1, tsm_bots 12.
  h9_mw_drainage   the sides start as on h9_up_drainage, with no zones. Mapconfig:
                   tsm_respawns 1, tsm_roundtime 420.

  Rebuilding them  in export-map\h9_drainage (node, SMAK beside TSM):
    node sky.mjs deploy          the sky (gfx\env\h9drain*.tga)
    node sprites.mjs deploy      the zone, flag, marker, rain and haze sprites
    node uplink.mjs deploy       models\tsm_uplink.mdl
    node drainage.mjs --compile deploy   all three maps, their .res and spawn configs
    node waypoints.mjs deploy    the bots' waypoints, under all three names
    node preview.mjs             a reel of stills and a flythrough with its sound

6. THE SKYLIFT  (export-map\h9_siege_lift)
------------------------------------------
A skylift car climbing its tether to orbit over the burning city: one car, four maps, as
the drainage works are three. The world outside streams down past the windows (conveyor
cloud cards, the tether, drifting cloud banks) under a still, toxic red sky; red radiated
haze clings to the gantry and the dock; the car groans, creaks and sways, everyone's view
rolling with it.

  h9_siege_lift  the siege. The Insurgents boarded underneath (the pod, with a dock off it)
                 and attack upward; MSR start on the top deck and keep the lift climbing. The
                 drive's fall stops the world outside.
  h9_up_lift     Capture the Uplink: zones 1 the clamp bay, 2 the power room, 3 the drive
                 room, 4 the mess; the uplink on the passenger deck. MSR on the drive deck,
                 the Insurgents in the pod and the cargo deck. Mapconfig: tsm_respawns 1,
                 tsm_roundtime 420.
  h9_ws_lift     Wave Survival: the survivors hold the passenger deck; the hordes come from
                 the pod, the cargo deck and the drive deck. Mapconfig: tsm_respawns 1,
                 tsm_bots 12.
  h9_mw_lift     Mech Warrior: the sides start as on h9_up_lift, with no zones. Mapconfig:
                 tsm_respawns 1, tsm_roundtime 420.
  The three mode maps have no siege objectives: the machines stand as scenery.

  Objectives     (the siege) 1 the tether clamps (cargo deck), 2 the power core (passenger
                 deck), 3 the drive (drive deck).
  Rooms          each machine in a walled room, four ways in: a door anyone opens; a door
                 only a Breacher forces (named tsm_breach_*); a door only a Combat Engineer
                 opens (tsm_eng_door); and a crawl vent behind a grate anyone can break.
  Routes         stairs between the decks; two maintenance shafts behind engineers' doors;
                 the gantry outside every deck, by its hatches and ladders (through holes in
                 the grating), open to everyone. Falling off it kills.
  Spawns         (the siege) MSR on the drive deck; Insurgents in the pod, then (forward
                 tiers) the cargo deck once the clamps fall and the passenger deck once the
                 power is cut.
  Mapconfig      (the siege) tsm_obj_team 2, the objective banners, tsm_respawns 1,
                 tsm_roundtime 480.

  Rebuilding it  in export-map\h9_siege_lift (node, SMAK beside TSM):
    node sky.mjs deploy          the sky (gfx\env\h9lift*.tga)
    smak sound sounds.json -o .. ..   its sounds, into TSM\sound\h9lift
    node sprites.mjs deploy      the haze sprite (sprites\h9_lift_haze.spr)
    node lift.mjs --compile deploy    the four maps, their .res and spawn configs
                                 (out\h9_lift.wad holds the scrolling textures, built into
                                 each .bsp; LIFT_ONLY=siege, up, ws or mw does one)
    node lift.mjs --views        pictures from inside, sky and all
    node waypoints.mjs deploy    the bots' waypoints, one walk under all four names
    node tactical.mjs            the tactical map: doors, shafts, hatches, ladders, spawns
