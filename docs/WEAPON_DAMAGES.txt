WEAPON DAMAGES -- the damage of every weapon, as the game builds it
=================================================================

Every number here is read from the weapon table the game compiles (reTS dlls\ts_weapon_info.cpp,
built into both mp.dll and client.dll): 40 weapons, TS 3.0's 38 (ids 0 to 37), the PPSh-41 (id 39), the laser rifle (id 40) and the Mech's rocket launcher (id 41, not in the table below: see its paragraph).
Id 38 is no game weapon: AMX Mod X's TSX uses it for the throwing knife (section 4), so the game's own
table leaves it empty. The table's damage is the shipped 'Sparta' value for the 32 firearms, which
overrides the figure in each weapon's own row; the melee, grenade and briefcase rows keep their own. A
server can change any of them with a weapons file (the 'customweapons' cvar, written by 'saveweapons');
this is the stock table.


1. THE FIREARMS, BY ID
----------------------

  Damage     what one bullet (one pellet, for a shotgun) does to a body, before range, armor and the
             whole-number cut below
  Pellets    bullets a shot sends (8 for every shotgun)
  Per shot   Damage x Pellets: the most one pull of the trigger does
  Rate       seconds between shots, as the table has it (a gun cannot fire faster)
  Dmg/s      Per shot / Rate: a ceiling, since spread, recoil, reloads and the trigger lower it
  Head       one bullet to the head: x2, or x1.5 for a shotgun pellet
  %UMP       Damage against the UMP's 31

  a minute, and a toggle between full auto and single fire (the fire-mode key switches, as on the AK47
  and the Uzi).

  The laser rifle (id 40) is an energy weapon that fires plasma: 35 damage, a little over the M4A1's 33, 800
  rounds a minute, 35 cells a pack (caliber 15, "Plasma Cell"), full auto, single and burst, and a bolt
  stops at the first thing it hits (pierce decay 0). Right click zooms to 32 and then 8 under a blue overlay,
  and it gunbashes for 35 like the M4A1.

  The Mech's rocket launcher (id 41) is no gun of the table: a shoulder-fired rocket launcher that only the Mech
  class carries (tsm_class9), in no buy menu and never dropped (the Mech's M60 neither: tsm_mech_nodrop; what he
  throws as he dies is taken out of the world). It fires a rocket that explodes on impact with radius damage. Its
  numbers are cvars of the game library, to be balanced by playing: h9_mechrocket_damage (150), h9_mechrocket_radius
  (200), h9_mechrocket_speed (900), h9_mechrocket_rate (1.5 seconds between shots), h9_mechrocket_clip (1). The
  blast also pushes the players it leaves alive, away from its middle and up, as hard as h9_mechrocket_push (400
  units a second) and h9_mechrocket_lift (250) with the damage's falloff (TS itself gives a living player no
  knockback from a hit); it spares a teammate when friendly fire is off and the thrower unless
  h9_mechrocket_selfdamage is 1, and a player it kills gets TS's own death launch. The
  old .rocket ability does 65 at the centre of a 150 unit blast, four a life; where the launcher exists .rocket
  only tells the player so. Its muzzle flash, smoke trail and explosion are sprites (sprites/h9_rocket_flash.spr,
  h9_rocket_smoke.spr, h9_rocket_boom.spr) that the plugin sends, each behind tsm_mech_rocket_flash, _smoke, _boom.


2. RANGE, AND WHAT A BULLET GOES THROUGH
---------------------------------------

A bullet keeps all its damage out to about ten range units of its caliber, then loses it as the
square root of the distance: it does 0.95 of its damage at 11 range units, 0.5 at 40 and 0.32 at 100
(a range unit is the number in the table below, in game units). A hit's damage is cut to a whole
number when it is taken.

    1  9 x 19mm                80              880             3200           0.2            30
    2  .45Acp                  90              990             3600           0.2            12
    3  SHELLS                  90              990             3600           0.0             8
    4  5.56 Nato              150             1650             6000           0.9            30
    5  .50AE                   90              990             3600           0.5            10
    6  7.62 x 39mm            170             1870             6800           0.7            30
    7  5.7 x 28               100             1100             4000           1.0            20
    8  50BMG                  200             2200             8000           1.0             5
    9  10mm AUTO               90              990             3600           0.5            15
   10  .22 LR                  70              770             2800           0.9            17
   11  .454 Casull             20              220              800          0.75             5
   12  .32Acp                  40              440             1600           0.0            20

A bullet that still has more than 5 damage after a hit goes on through up to 48 units of material
and hits once more, as one bullet with its damage times the pierce decay (half again if it went
through a player). A decay of 0 never goes through: every shotgun's shells, the .32 and the laser rifle's plasma.


3. WHAT CHANGES A HIT
--------------------

  Head                 x2 for a bullet, x1.5 for a shotgun pellet; a blade or a fist gets nothing
  Chest, stomach, arms x1: there is no torso multiplier
  Legs                 x0.5
  Kevlar               x0.7875 (the server's armor_absorb, 0.2125) on the chest, stomach and arms
                       for a bullet; the head and the legs are not covered, and neither is a blade,
                       a fall or an explosion
  Bullet time dodge    x0.7 for a bullet taken during the 0.55 second dodge
  Stunt and slow-mo    x0.7 for a player in a stunt or slow-motion state
  Spawn protection     no damage at all for one second after a spawn
  Friendly fire        no damage between teammates while mp_friendlyfire is off

They multiply. A pistol bullet to the head of a player in kevlar is a head hit, so the kevlar does
not count; to the chest it is x0.7875; to a leg it is x0.5.


4. MELEE, THROWN BLADES, THE GRENADE, THE BRIEFCASE
---------------------------------------------------

A knife or katana blow is scaled by the melee bar (0 to 100): x(bar x 0.5 + 50) / 100, so x1.0 at a
full bar and x0.5 at an empty one (the kung-fu blows are flat). A fist or a blade gets no head bonus.

  ID  Weapon          Blow                                      Damage (at a full melee bar)
  --  --------------  ----------------------------------------  --------------------------------
   0  Kung-fu         punch                                     35 (flat: not scaled by the bar)
                      kick                                      45
                      back kick                                 55
  25  Combat Knife    slash                                     30 (20 while stunting)
                      thrown, touching a player                 100
  35  Seal Knife      slash                                     20
                      thrown, touching a player                 50
  34  Katana          slash, on the ground                      45
                      slash, ducking                            65
                      slash while moving backward               75, swung behind the player
                      slash in the air                          speed x 2/13, from 35 to 55
                      slash during a stunt                      35
                      thrown, touching a player                 150
  38  Throwing Knife  any thrown blade that touches a player; TSX's id for it, not a game weapon:
                      a thrown Combat Knife                     100
                      a thrown Seal Knife                       50
                      a thrown Katana                           150
                      a thrown blade in flight, on glass and    0.1 x its speed
                      what it can kill (never a player)
  24  M61 Grenade     explosion                                 110 to everything within 300 units,
                                                                less out to nothing at 1024
  29  Briefcase       (the capture the flag objective)          0

A thrown blade or grenade in flight shatters what it crosses that it can kill (glass and the like,
never a player), taking 0.1 x its speed as the damage.

Weapon 38, the Throwing Knife, is how AMX Mod X's TSX names any thrown blade: a kill or a hit made with one
reaches plugins (client_death, client_damage) as weapon 38, whichever knife was thrown, and a kill with it
is worth 2 points, as a kung-fu kill is (every other weapon's is worth 1). It is not a game weapon: the
game's own ids are 0 to 37, the PPSh-41's 39, the laser rifle's 40 and the Mech launcher's 41, so ts_giveweapon never takes 38.

6. WHERE THE NUMBERS LIVE
-------------------------

  reTS\dlls\ts_weapon_info.cpp   the table: each weapon's row (Write), its combat feel (Sparta())
                                 and the ammo (set_ammo); shared by mp.dll and client.dll
  reTS\dlls\TS_combat.cpp        the range falloff and the pierce (ShootBulletsPlayer)
  reTS\dlls\player.cpp           the hit-location multipliers, kevlar and the dodge (TraceAttack,
                                 TakeDamage)
  reTS\dlls\ts_gun.cpp           the melee blows (KnifeFire, KatanaFire, CCombatAttack)
  reTS\dlls\ts_grenades.cpp      the grenade and the thrown blades
