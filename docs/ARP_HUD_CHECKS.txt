THE ARP HUD PANELS -- TO CHECK (docs\APOLLO_RP.txt 2 says what they are)
======================================================================

(nothing here has been seen in a game; each step names what to look at)

A. Deploy (the lead's builds): ARP_Core.amxx and ARP_HUD.amxx together (build.bat apollo), and the client.dll with h9_arphud.cpp;
   web client through webclient-sync. The new message is registered in plugin_precache, so change the map (or restart the server)
   after the plugins are in place.

B. Server only, nobody at a screen (the point: no client needed to know what the server does).
   1. AMX Mod X log on h9_tsrp_hive: no ARP error; no "native not found".
   2. Join with a bot, an older H9 game and any other game: arp_hudstate lists the bot as "a bot, sent no HUD", the older H9 game as
      TEXT HUD with "his game sends no _h9arphud" and the other game as TEXT HUD with "his game draws no H9 panels". The log has the
      "[ARP] HUD: <name> is sent the text HUD: ..." line with the reason for each human.
   3. h9_overflow.log (ts\logs): note whether it names "Datagram overflowed" for human players. If it does, the old text HUD
      is dropped with the datagram on this map and the panel (reliable) is the way round it; if it does not, that is not the cause.

C. With H9's client on h9_tsrp_hive.
   1. Within a few seconds of the join: log "[ARP] HUD: <you> is sent the HUD panel: _h9arphud is 1" and "[ARP] HUD panel: first update
      sent to <you> (HUD 1: 7 line(s) ...)"; arp_hudstate shows PANEL, "_h9arphud is 1" and the last lines "<< city >>| |Wallet: $..|Bank..".
      If it says TEXT HUD with "sends no _h9arphud" while his cl_h9_arphud is 1, his userinfo is full: arp_hudstate shows the key as
      missing; shorten something else in the userinfo (docs\CUSTOM_XASH_USERID.txt, budget).
   2. On the screen: a purple panel at the top left with the city as the heading and a rail under it; Wallet, Bank, Payday, Salary and
      Job under, each label dimmer than its value. cl_h9_arphud_list in the console: key "1", messages read > 0, HUD 1 shown.
      If arp_hudstate says PANEL and messages were sent but cl_h9_arphud_list says "0 message(s) read": the message does not reach the
      game (hook or network); if it says some read but "no lines held" or another reason, the reason is the thing to read.
   3. Earn or spend money: Wallet changes within two seconds. Walk into another zone: Location changes. Eat or starve (hunger on): the line
      appears. Wait on the spot a minute: the panel does not blink (the refresh sends the same lines and changes nothing).
   4. Each switch: cl_h9_arphud_wallet 0 (the wallet line goes, the rest stay), _title 0 (the heading and the blank go, no empty heading),
      _job 0, _status 0 then die (only the heading is left of the panel while dead, the line that says so is gone), _extra 0 (hunger, location go),
      every kind 0 (the panel goes), then back to 1. cl_h9_arphud_left 0: the whole panel goes and no text takes its place.
   5. cl_h9_arphud 0: within two seconds arp_hudstate says TEXT HUD ("_h9arphud is 0"), the panel is gone and the text HUD (if the game
      draws it at all) returns; 1: the panel is back within two seconds. cl_h9_vgui 0 does the same. arp_hud_panel 0 on the server: everyone
      is TEXT HUD ("arp_hud_panel is 0 on this server"), even with cl_h9_arphud 1; 1: panels again.
   6. Dead or spectating: the panel holds the title and "You are currently dead/spectating..." only.
   7. Open /menu, /items, a shop: a menu at the left (the panel menu from a third of the height, the text menu centred) and the HUD panel
      above it stays if it ends above a quarter of the height, else it goes and returns when the menu closes; the buy menu and the scoreboard
      hide it; nohud 1 hides it.
   8. cl_h9_vgui_scale 0.5 and 2, and a resize: the panel follows; no gap in the art at any size. Try 640x480 and 1920x1080.
   9. arp_ooc 2 and a "(( hello ))" in chat: the third HUD appears at the right, its first line as a heading, then the lines; two lines
      from two players stack; it does not cover the kill feed. Look at another player or a property with arp_hud4_type 2: a short notice
      under the crosshair box ("Name: ... Job: ..."), gone three seconds after you look away.
   10. A long hostname (set hostname to 80 characters, change the map): the title wraps inside the panel, nothing runs past the art.
       The default title's guillemets: shown as quotation marks if the font has them, else left out (unverified).
   11. Other plugins' lines: jail someone (Jailed: No Salary, Cuffed), drink (Blood Alcohol Level): they are _extra lines.
   12. Web client: the same, with its own cl_h9_arphud_list.

D. What to send back if it fails: the AMX log lines "[ARP] HUD ...", arp_hudstate, cl_h9_arphud_list, and ts\logs\h9_overflow.log.
