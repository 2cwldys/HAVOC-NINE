THE ARP HUD PANELS -- TO CHECK (docs\APOLLO_RP.txt 2 says what they are)
======================================================================

(nothing here has been seen in a game; each step names what to look at)

A. Deploy (the lead's builds): ARP_Core.amxx and ARP_HUD.amxx together (build.bat apollo), and the client.dll with h9_arphud.cpp;
   web client through webclient-sync. The new message is registered in plugin_precache, so change the map (or restart the server)
   after the plugins are in place.

B. Server only, nobody at a screen (the point: no client needed to know what the server does).
   1. AMX Mod X log on h9_tsrp_hive: no ARP error; no "native not found".
   2. Join with a bot, an older H9 game and any other game: arp_hudstate lists the bot as "a bot, sent no HUD", the older H9 game as
      TEXT HUD with "his game sends no _h9arphud" and the other game as TEXT HUD with "his game draws no H9 panels". The log has the
      "[ARP] HUD: <name> is sent the text HUD: ..." line with the reason for each human.
   3. h9_overflow.log (ts\logs): note whether it names "Datagram overflowed" for human players. If it does, the old text HUD
      is dropped with the datagram on this map and the panel (reliable) is the way round it; if it does not, that is not the cause.

C. With H9's client on h9_tsrp_hive.
   1. Within a few seconds of the join: log "[ARP] HUD: <you> is sent the HUD panel: _h9arphud is 1" and "[ARP] HUD panel: first update
      sent to <you> (HUD 1: 7 line(s) ...)"; arp_hudstate shows PANEL, "_h9arphud is 1" and the last lines "<< city >>| |Wallet: $..|Bank..".
      If it says TEXT HUD with "sends no _h9arphud" while his cl_h9_arphud is 1, his userinfo is full: arp_hudstate shows the key as
      missing; shorten something else in the userinfo (docs\CUSTOM_XASH_USERID.txt, budget).
   2. On the screen: a purple panel at the top left with the city as the heading and a rail under it; Wallet, Bank, Payday, Salary and
      Job under, each label dimmer than its value. cl_h9_arphud_list in the console: key "1", messages read > 0, HUD 1 shown.
      If arp_hudstate says PANEL and messages were sent but cl_h9_arphud_list says "0 message(s) read": the message does not reach the
      game (hook or network); if it says some read but "no lines held" or another reason, the reason is the thing to read.
   3. Earn or spend money: Wallet changes within two seconds. Walk into another zone: Location changes. Eat or starve (hunger on): the Needs panel
      at the top right changes (13). Wait on the spot a minute: the panel does not blink (the refresh sends the same lines and changes nothing).
   4. Each switch: cl_h9_arphud_wallet 0 (the wallet line goes, the rest stay), _title 0 (the heading and the blank go, no empty heading),
      _job 0, _status 0 then die (only the heading is left of the panel while dead, the line that says so is gone), _extra 0 (location goes),
      every kind 0 (the panel goes), then back to 1. cl_h9_arphud_left 0: the whole panel goes and no text takes its place.
   5. cl_h9_arphud 0: within two seconds arp_hudstate says TEXT HUD ("_h9arphud is 0"), the panel is gone and the text HUD (if the game
      draws it at all) returns; 1: the panel is back within two seconds. cl_h9_vgui 0 does the same. arp_hud_panel 0 on the server: everyone
      is TEXT HUD ("arp_hud_panel is 0 on this server"), even with cl_h9_arphud 1; 1: panels again.
   6. Dead or spectating: the panel holds the title and "You are currently dead/spectating..." only.
   7. Open /menu, /items, a shop: a menu at the left (the panel menu from a third of the height, the text menu centred) and the HUD panel
      above it stays if it ends above a quarter of the height, else it goes and returns when the menu closes; the buy menu and the scoreboard
      hide it; nohud 1 hides it.
   8. cl_h9_vgui_scale 0.5 and 2, and a resize: the panel follows; no gap in the art at any size. Try 640x480 and 1920x1080.
   9. arp_ooc 2 and a "(( hello ))" in chat: the third HUD appears at the right, its first line as a heading, then the lines; two lines
      from two players stack; it does not cover the kill feed. Look at another player or a property with arp_hud4_type 2: a short notice
      under the crosshair box ("Name: ... Job: ..."), gone three seconds after you look away.
   10. A long hostname (set hostname to 80 characters, change the map): the title wraps inside the panel, nothing runs past the art.
       The default title's guillemets: shown as quotation marks if the font has them, else left out (unverified).
   11. Other plugins' lines: jail someone (Jailed: No Salary, Cuffed), drink (Blood Alcohol Level): they are _extra lines.
   12. Web client: the same, with its own cl_h9_arphud_list.
   13. The needs (hunger). With the live database backed up, on the server arp_hunger_enabled 1 (the shipped arp.cfg has 1; if the server has set it
      to 0, set it in the server console and change the map) and join on h9_tsrp_hive as a living player. Within a few seconds a purple panel headed "Needs" at
      the top right (from about 104 px down, clear of the round time and the kill feed) with one row: Hunger, a bar and the number. A new
      character starts at 0%: the bar is empty and the number has the normal text colour. cl_h9_arphud_list names cl_h9_arphud_needs, and
      arp_hudstate shows "HUD 2: sent ...: Hunger: 0%". Hunger rises by 1 about every 90 seconds, so to see the rest set it
      in the database: stop the server, back up data\sqlite3\arp.sq3, UPDATE arp_users SET hunger = 400 WHERE authid = '<yours>' (the
      column is tenths of a per cent: 400 is 40%), start the server and join. 40%: the bar filled two fifths in a yellow-green. 74%
      (740): nearly red, the number still normal. 75% (750): the bar red, the number red, the chat says you are starting to get very
      hungry and you walk slower. 90% (900): a red row "Starving" under the bar a second or two after the join (the very hungry message comes first), a chat line, no salary at the payday. 100%
      (1000): the bar full. Two thin light marks cross the bar at three quarters and nine tenths. Eat (/items: the Steak heals 80, the
      Soda 5): the bar falls by 5 each second and the colour goes back with it, within two seconds of each change.
      Thirst: with arp_thirst_enabled 1 on the server (the shipped arp.cfg has 1) a second row, Thirst, stands under Hunger. It rises by 1 about
      every 45 seconds and is not stored (a player who joins starts at 0, and one who dies starts again at 0, as hunger does). A Soda or a Milk lowers it by
      35 or 30 when it is finished, also with no hunger to heal. From 90 a red row "Dehydrated" stands under the bars. It changes nothing else: no
      slowdown, no message, no salary. Let it climb, die (or have an admin kill you) and respawn: both rows are back at 0%.
      Dropped items: drop an item (/items, or the inventory window) and look at it from a few steps: the notice names it ("Soda x3"), and its picture
      stands above the notice, the same picture as its tile in the inventory window. cl_h9_arphud_icon 0 takes the picture away and leaves the notice;
      with arp_h9_itemhover 0 on the server nothing is said of the item at all. A game that says _h9vgui 5 gets the text without the picture.
      A. arp_hunger_enabled 0 on the server: the panel is gone within two seconds (arp_hudstate says "HUD 2: taken down"), and no empty panel is left.
      B. cl_h9_arphud_needs 0: the panel goes at once and nothing takes its place (not as text); 1 brings it back. cl_h9_arphud_top 0 does
         the same, and cl_h9_arphud_left 0 does not touch it.
      C. Die, or spectate: the panel is gone (the first panel says you are dead); respawn: it returns.
      D. cl_h9_arphud 0 (the text HUD): "Hunger: 40%" flush right at the top of the screen in the second HUD's colour, not among the lines
         at the left, and from 90 "Starving" under it. cl_h9_arphud 1: the panel again.
      E. cl_h9_vgui_scale 0.5 and 2, 640x480 and 1920x1080: the label is whole, the bar keeps a width, nothing runs out of the panel.
      Wrong: the panel with no bar (a line of text "Hunger: 40%" in the panel: the game is older than the server), the bar green at 80, a
      panel with nothing in it, the panel still there after death or after arp_hunger_enabled 0.
   14. The weapon bar and the left panel. With the default cl_showequipment 1 (a faint row of the weapons carried at the top left of the
      screen, five columns: melee and grenades, pistols, sub machine guns, rifles and shotguns, akimbo guns) the first panel starts
      under the lowest weapon over the columns it covers, with a small gap: carry a pistol and a rifle and it stands just under their row.
      Pick up more guns of one kind (the pistols are the second column from the left): the panel slides down a row as you pick them up,
      and back up when you drop them. Open the weapon select (the mouse wheel, or a key 1 to 5): the panel does not move but is drawn smaller (a
      smaller font, three quarters of the art; cl_h9_arphud_shrink 0 leaves its size), and does not move when the select closes, where it
      has its size again. cl_showequipment 2 and 3: the same. cl_showequipment 0 (no bar until the select opens): the panel
      stands at the top, slides down within a fifth of a second of the select opening, clear of the bright bar, and back up when it shuts.
      Dead, spectating, nohud 1 or the buy menu: the bar is gone and the panel slides to the top. The panels at the right (the needs,
      the chat) never move with the bar. With so many weapons that the panel cannot stand under the bar, or on a low screen (at 640x480
      the safe area is about 300 px tall), the panel is held inside the safe area, 60 px and seven lines of text above the bottom edge, and
      covers the bar's lowest rows: that is the rule. Wrong: a jump of more than a few pixels in one frame, the panel moving when the
      select opens with cl_showequipment 1 to 3, the panel over the weapons' pictures while there is room under them, a panel running
      off the bottom of the screen, the panel at its full size while the select is open (with cl_h9_arphud_shrink 1), or still small
      after the select has shut.
