CAPTURE THE FLAG -- H9's own flag mode: how it plays, every number you can change, how to make a map
=====================================================================================================

ts_ctf.amxx on the ts_matchmode core. Maps named h9_ctf_<name>. Its cvars are in configs\ts_ctf.cfg (and the
three presets' copies in H9XASH\presets). This is the reference for the mode; docs\GAME_MODES.txt says what
each kind of map plays and how a map is set up in general.

Two things called capture the flag exist, and they are not the same game:

  * h9_ctf_<name> (this file): H9's own. The match mode with its nine classes, teams, a flag on each side's
    pad, respawn waves with no lives to run out, marks through the walls.
  * ctf_<name>, ctb_<name>, h9_ctb_<name> and any name holding _ctf_ or _ctb_ (ctb_deadheat, ts_ctf_rooftops):
    TS's OWN capture game, its flag the briefcase. It plays as the game ships (section 7): no match mode, the
    buy menu, TS's own rules. This plugin takes none of those maps.


1. WHAT IT IS
-------------

Each side has a flag standing on its own flag pad. A player takes the ENEMY's flag by walking over it and
carries it to his own pad: standing on his pad with the enemy flag on his back (and, by default, his own flag
at home) is a capture. The first side to tsm_cf_caps (3) captures wins; at the clock the side with more wins,
equal is a draw. Nobody is ever counted out: every death waits for the side's next respawn wave (20 seconds
apart), so the flags decide, not the fighting alone. By default the first round that ends is the whole match.


2. HOW IT PLAYS
---------------

The flags. A flag is a banner on a pole (models\tsm_flag.mdl, made with SMAK by export-map\h9_drainage\flag.mjs)
standing on its pad, and it CHANGES COLOUR WITH POSSESSION: at its pad it wears its own side's colour (side 1's
blue, side 2's red); an enemy who carries it turns it to the carrier's colour; where it lies, dropped, it is
grey. Carried, it is worn up the carrier's back. Flags exist only while a round is live; the pad rings and BASE
marks stay up all map.

Taking one. Every tenth of a second each flag that is not carried looks for the NEAREST living player in play
within 48 units across the floor (and 64 up or down of the flag's middle). If he is on the enemy side of the
flag he takes it, from its pad or from where it lies. The nearest player decides: a defender standing nearer a
flag at its pad than an attacker keeps the grab from him. The player who just dropped a flag cannot take it
again for 1.5 seconds.

Carrying. The carrier keeps EVERY weapon, is marked to BOTH sides, and scores a point (tsm_score_carry) for
every tsm_score_carry_secs (15) seconds he carries. He can still take back his own side's dropped flag by
walking over it. He lets the flag go with "dropflag" in the console (bind a key to it) or .drop in chat, and it
falls at his feet; if he dies, is eliminated, or leaves, it drops where he was, grey, with half his velocity
and a toss.

Returning. A player of a flag's own side who is nearest to it when it lies dropped takes it back at once to its
pad, for tsm_score_return (3) points. A dropped flag nobody touches flies home tsm_cf_return (20) seconds after
it was dropped (0: it stays where it lies). A flag that lands in a wall, the sky or out of the world goes home
at once.

Capturing. The carrier of the enemy's flag who comes within tsm_cf_pad_radius (72) units of his own pad (and
within 72 up or down of its floor) captures at once, with no hold time, provided his own flag is at its pad
(tsm_cf_needhome 1; 0 lets him score whatever has become of it). If it is not, nothing happens and a human is
told "Your own flag is not at your pad -- get it back first." A capture gives the carrier tsm_score_capture
(10) points, which are also his side's, counts one capture for his side, puts the captured flag back on its
pad, and (tsm_cf_capture_wave 1) brings his side's waiting dead back at once, except when it wins the round.
The side whose flag was taken gets nothing.

Winning. The first side to tsm_cf_caps captures wins the round at once. At the clock (tsm_roundtime: 300 by
default, 600 on h9_ctf_drainage) the side with more captures wins and equal is a draw; there is no overtime and
no sudden death. With tsm_roundtime 0 only captures end a round. h9_ctf_rounds 0 (the default): the first round
that ends is the whole match, its winner the match's, a draw a drawn match, the bar and the chat saying MATCH;
1 plays rounds, best of tsm_rounds. The cvar is read every tenth of a second.

Lives. None are counted. Both sides respawn in waves every tsm_cf_wave (20) seconds, and players waiting for a
wave count as alive, so no side can be eliminated and the round never ends that way. A dead player waits at
least tsm_respawn_seconds (5) for a timed wave, so the wait is between 5 and about 25 seconds, plus TS's own
death timer. They come back at their side's base with the full class kit, the Officer with his kung fu. A death costs
only the wait, and for a carrier the flag; an Officer's fall is not announced to both sides ("officer has
perished", tsm_officerslain_sound) in this mode.

The classes and the buying. As on every H9 match map: the nine classes with their loadouts, no buy menu (the
match mode holds everyone's cash at zero and weaponrestriction is 1).

Bots play the flags while tsm_bot_objectives is 1 (the default): they take the enemy's flag, carry it to their own
pad, bring their own dropped flag back, escort the carrier and guard their flag. A bot cannot use dropflag (a fake
client sends no client commands), so it lets a flag go only by dying. With tsm_bot_objectives 0 they fight and neither
take nor return flags, unless tsm_cf_bots is 1 (then a bot that walks over a flag takes it, returns it and carries it,
like anyone). The fields rcbot reads are in docs\BOT_OBJECTIVES.txt.


3. THE NUMBERS YOU CAN CHANGE
-----------------------------

Where to set them: configs\ts_ctf.cfg, the presets in H9XASH\presets, the map's own ts\mapconfigs\<map>.cfg, or
the server console. "Read" says when a change counts.

     tsm_ctf_mode               1   the mode on: a map with both pads is claimed as a flag map (0: an h9_ctf_
                                    map plays as plain team elimination); read when the map is claimed, 2 seconds
                                    after it loads, and again when the second pad is placed
     tsm_cf_caps                3   captures that win the round (live)
     tsm_cf_return             20   seconds before a dropped flag flies home; 0: it stays (live)
     tsm_cf_needhome            1   1: a carrier scores only while his own flag is at its pad (live)
     tsm_cf_pad_radius         72   how near its pad a carrier must be, units across the floor (live; the ring
                                    on the floor is sized once when it is made)
     tsm_cf_capture_wave        1   1: the capturing side's waiting dead come back at once (live)
     tsm_cf_wave               20   seconds between both sides' waves (from the next round start; the core's
                                    tsm_respawn_wave is not used while this is above 0)
     tsm_cf_bots                0   1: a bot that walks over a flag takes it (live)
     h9_ctf_rounds              0   0: one round is the match; 1: rounds, best of tsm_rounds
     tsm_gamedesc_ctf  Capture the Flag   the game's name in the server browser and on the Discord card
     tsm_roundtime            300   the round clock (the shipped map's mapconfig sets 600); 0: none
     tsm_rounds                 9   best-of, only with h9_ctf_rounds 1
     tsm_respawn_seconds      5.0   a timed wave takes only those dead at least this long
     tsm_score_capture         10   points for a capture (the carrier's and his side's)
     tsm_score_return           3   points for taking a dropped flag home
     tsm_score_carry            1   points for every tsm_score_carry_secs seconds carried
     tsm_score_carry_secs      15   (the counter is the uplink's and carries over from one carry to the next)
     tsm_freezetime             6   seconds frozen before the round goes live; there are no flags in the freeze
     tsm_verbosity              0   0 silences every [MATCH] chat line (the mode's too); 1 shows them
     tsm_bots                  10   bots the server keeps; any bot in a match means it is not rated

Chat is silent as shipped: at tsm_verbosity 0 a flag taken, dropped or returned and a capture are told by the
news banners, the carrier's status and the marks (H9 games) or the markers, the centre texts and the sounds
(any other game).


4. COMMANDS
-----------

Players:
     dropflag (console, bind it) or .drop / say_team .drop (chat)   let the flag go where you stand. Anyone who
                         carries nothing is told so. Only the carrier's .drop line is swallowed.

Admins (ADMIN_CFG):
     amx_tsm_ctf                      the mode's state: build, whether it claimed the map, the captures, the rules
                                      in force, the flag model and rings found, the pads and where the flags are
     amx_tsm_cf_pad <1|2>             that side's flag pad at your feet; both pads make the map a flag map on the spot
     amx_tsm_cf_list                  the pads (from the config or the map) and each flag: at its pad, carried, dropped
     amx_tsm_cf_save                  write configs\tsm_ctf_<map>.cfg (replaces the file; pads the map's entities define are written too)
     tsm_cf_pad <side> <x> <y> <z>    the server command that file is made of (hand edits are fine)
     amx_tsm_cf_give <flag side> <0|1|2>   to test, in a live round: give that side's flag to a person of the side given
                                      (a human before a bot; nothing checks it is the enemy), or 0: put it home
     amx_tsm_cf_tp <side> <pad 1|2>   to test: that side's carrier (else its first person in play) walks onto a pad:
                                      amx_tsm_cf_give 2 1 then amx_tsm_cf_tp 1 1 is a capture


5. WHAT PLAYERS SEE
-------------------

Through the walls: every flag and every pad, on every screen. An H9 game draws a badge in the colour of whoever
holds it: FLAG at its pad, CARRIED over the carrier (following him), DROPPED where it lies with a bar draining to
the moment it flies home, BASE on each pad (cl_h9_marks; docs\H9_VGUI.txt). Any other game gets TS's own
objective marker: "THE MSR FLAG", "THE MSR FLAG - CARRIED", "THE MSR FLAG - DROPPED", "MSR BASE".

The match bar reads "MATCH -- 8:12 -- CAPTURES 1 - 0 (FIRST TO 3)" over "MSR -- INS". The carrier of an H9 game
gets a status under the bar, "YOU HAVE THE ENEMY FLAG -- TAKE IT TO YOUR PAD", or while his own flag is away
"YOUR FLAG IS AWAY -- GET IT BACK, THEN SCORE"; any other game a centre text. Every H9 game gets a 3-second
banner for each event (has the flag, dropped, took it back, captured), blue for side 1, red for side 2.
Sounds: a pick-up, a drop, a return, and a bell for a capture heard map-wide. A dead player reads "RESPAWN WAVE
IN 12s", with no count of lives, and the class menu says "respawns".

A flag without its model (models\tsm_flag.mdl missing) falls back to the briefcase tinted by a coloured glow.


6. MAKING A FLAG MAP
--------------------

1. Name it h9_ctf_<name>. A copy of an Uplink map keeps that map's zones: put tsm_uplink_mode 0 in its mapconfig
   (h9_ctf_drainage has it). No tsm_obj_ siege objectives on it.
2. The pads: info_target entities named tsm_flag_1 and tsm_flag_2 at the FLOOR where the flag should stand, or in
   the game: stand on each, amx_tsm_cf_pad <1|2>, then amx_tsm_cf_save (configs\tsm_ctf_<map>.cfg). A pad from the
   config beats the map's entity of the same side. Put each at the rear of its side's base on flat, open floor with
   room for the carrier to be seen and to reach it. A first proposal: node tools\assault-spawns\cli.mjs <map> --pads
   --overview <folder>.
3. The spawns: configs\tsm_spawns_<map>.cfg, each side's base (tier 0). Flag maps open no spawn sets.
4. A mapconfig, ts\mapconfigs\<map>.cfg: the round time (600 is a fair start). rcbot waypoints for the bots.
5. models\tsm_flag.mdl and sprites\tsm_zone_ring_1.spr and _2.spr: without the rings there is no ring and no bell.
6. tools\make_assault_maps.py makes h9_ctf_drainage from h9_up_drainage; its flag model is built by
   export-map\h9_drainage\flag.mjs (node flag.mjs deploy): two bodies (standing, worn), three skins (grey, blue,
   red), one idle sequence.

For a bot author: a flag is the entity classname tsm_flag (pev_team: whose flag; pev_iuser1: 0 at its pad, 1 carried,
2 dropped; pev_owner: the carrier while carried) and a pad's ring is classname tsm_flagpad (pev_team its side).
The flag fields iuser2, fuser2 and vuser1 are in docs\BOT_OBJECTIVES.txt.


7. TS'S OWN CAPTURE GAME
------------------------

TS's flag is the briefcase (the ts_briefcase entity, one a side), and the stock game has one such mode: a map named
ctb_ or ctf_ at the start, or holding _ctb_ or _ctf_, or h9_ctb_ (ctb_deadheat is the worked example) plays it. The
H9 match mode stays out entirely, as on any map the game ships with: team deathmatch rules, no warmup or ready-up,
no side or class menus, no bots of its own unless tsm_briefcase_bots asks for some (tsm_bots 0
otherwise), TS's own cash, powerups AND weapon rules
(weaponrestriction 0), so the buy menu works. The flag is TS's own, with its own rules. The Discord card, the site
and the server browser call such a map Capture the Briefcase (ctb_) or Capture the Flag (ctf_). A mapconfig line
(tsm_enabled 1) makes any map a match map whatever its name says.

Bots play it. With tsm_briefcase_bots N in ts_matchmode.cfg (or a tsm_bots line in the map's mapconfig) the map gets N
bots, and rcbot_mm.dll plays TS's briefcase game with them while tsm_bot_stockobjectives is not 0: they fetch the
other side's briefcase, carry it to their own base, bring their own back, escort the carrier, guard their base and
hunt the other side's carrier (docs\BOT_OBJECTIVES.txt, section 5). It needs two bases (ownerteam 1 and 2) and rcbot's
waypoints for the map.


8. RANKING, THE CARD, THE SITE
------------------------------

The match is on the MATCH ladder (docs\H9_COMPETITIVE.txt) and is rated as the single round it is when
h9_ctf_rounds is 0: the server says so (oneRound), and havoc9.org asks one round and the five minutes. A drawn
match and a match with a bot in it rate nobody (turn tsm_bots off for a ranked server).

The server publishes tsm_pres_mode "ctf" and tsm_pres_prog "1/0/3" (side 1's captures, side 2's, the captures that
win). The Discord card reads "Capture the Flag - captures: MSR 1, Insurgents 0 (first to 3)" (a game older than
2026-11-05 names the mode from the map but shows no captures); the in-game server browser's Mode column and
havoc9.org read "Capture the Flag". The captures are not kept per player; the points are the scoreboard's.


9. THE SHIPPED MAP
------------------

h9_ctf_drainage, a copy of h9_up_drainage: side 1 starts at the north end, side 2 at the south, 34 spawn points
each, and the pads at the rear of each base: side 1 at (8, 1458.8, -11.8), side 2 at (8, -1442.8, -11.8), 2900
units apart. A first pass, to be walked. Its mapconfig: tsm_uplink_mode 0, tsm_respawns 1, tsm_roundtime 600.


10. THINGS TO KNOW
------------------

 * tsm_ctf_mode is read when the map is claimed; setting it to 0 later does not release the map.
 * The captures are reset when the next round's freeze begins, so the card keeps the last result through warmup.
 * Moving a pad during a round moves its ring, mark and capture test, but a flag already standing at the old
   spot stays there until it next goes home or the next round starts.
 * Both flags can be away at once; with tsm_cf_needhome 1 neither carrier can score until one is returned.
 * A friendly player nearer to a dropped flag than an enemy returns it instead of letting the enemy take it.
 * Handing a flag over with dropflag beside a teammate works as a pass: the dropper is locked out for 1.5 seconds
   and the teammate takes it if he is the nearest.
