===============================================================================
 APOLLO RP: EVERY COMMAND AND WHAT IT DOES
===============================================================================

 For a player on a roleplay map (h9_tsrp_hive, h9_tsrp_newbyzantium), a police officer, and the ARP admin who runs the server. Written from the
 plugin sources (apollo-rp\base\addons\amxmodx\scripting) and configs\arp\arp.cfg. The server's own list of what ARP has registered is "arp_help"
 in the game console; docs\ARP_ADMINS.txt says how to become an ARP admin; docs\APOLLO_RP.txt and docs\ARP_INVENTORY.txt are the rest.

 CONTENTS
    1. How to read this
    2. Player commands (chat)
    3. Chat, radio and phone
    4. Police commands
    5. Property commands
    6. ARP admin commands (console)
    7. Server console only: the database

 1. HOW TO READ THIS
 -------------------
   * A command that starts with / is typed in chat: press y, type it, Enter. A command with no slash is typed in the console (~), or by rcon.
   * <user> is a part of a player's name, or #<userid>: the number right after the quoted name in the server's "status" (docs\ARP_ADMINS.txt, 2).
     A name part that fits two players is refused.
   * Access: a job gives rights as letters. a is police, b medical, z is an ARP admin (arp_police_access, arp_medical_access, arp_admin_access).
     The server's console and rcon are always let in to the admin commands.
   * "looking at" means the crosshair is on that player or door, within the range given (200 units unless it says otherwise).
   * Money is the wallet (what you carry; you lose it when you die with arp_wallet_death 1) or the bank (safe, paid your salary every minute).

 2. PLAYER COMMANDS (chat)
 -------------------------
   /items, /inventory     your items: the inventory window in an H9 game, a text menu in any other. docs\ARP_INVENTORY.txt.
   /menu, arp_menu        ARP's client menu.
   /buy                   buys the property (door) you are looking at, if it is for sale; it takes the price from your BANK.
   /givemoney <amount>    gives that much of your wallet to the player you are looking at.
   /withdraw <amount>     takes money out of your bank into your wallet: facing a banker NPC, or anywhere if you carry a Debit Card.
   /deposit <amount>      puts wallet money into the bank, facing a banker.
   /transfer <user> <amount>   sends bank money to another player's bank, facing a banker.
   /unemploy              leaves your job; you are unemployed.
   /holster, holster      puts your weapon away, or takes it out again.
   /tazer                 fires a tazer you carry at the player you look at (the item Tazer; it needs the item).
   /removetape            takes the tape off the mouth of the player you look at (within 100 units); see Tape below.
   /untie                 frees the player you look at from his rope (within 100 units).
   /cuff                  cuffs, or frees, the player you look at (200 units). Police need no item; anyone else needs one Cuffs, which the cuffing
                          uses up and which goes back to whoever frees him unless police took them off. Cops cannot be cuffed.
   The use key (E) on a player: offers him an exchange of goods; on a handcuffed or jailed player it searches him instead, and you can take his
   items and money (docs\ARP_INVENTORY.txt, 6 and 9).
   Items that do something when used (in the inventory window: double-click, or right-click Use): Cuffs (as /cuff), Tape (gags the player you look
   at, within 100 units, and is used up; he cannot talk in chat, team chat or, with arp_voice 1, by voice, until /removetape, his death or leaving),
   Rope (ties the player you look at to you; he is pulled along when he is further than arp_h9_rope_length (150) units, the rope snaps at four
   times that, and ends with either's death or leaving; used again on him it frees him, as does /untie), Lockpick and Electric Lockpick, Flashbang,
   Chlorofoam, Door C2 and Steel Axe (door breakers), Bug and Bug Scanner, phones, food, drink and drugs, weapons (using a gun gives it).

 3. CHAT, RADIO AND PHONE
 ------------------------
   /me <action>  (also /action, /m)   says what you do: "* Name <action>" to the players near you (normal talking range, times arp_message_range).
                          On a roleplay map an H9 game also draws it over your head in red, as well as in chat.
   /shout <text>  (/s)    speaks twice as far as normal.
   /whisper <text>  (/quiet, /w, /q)   speaks half as far.
   /ooc <text>            out of character, to everybody (arp_ooc: 0 off, 1 in chat, 2 in a box on the right of the screen).
   /cnn <text>            a news headline to everybody, once every arp_cnn seconds per server (0 turns it off).
   /advert <text>         an advertisement to everybody, once every arp_advert seconds (0 turns it off).
   /com <text>            police only: a message to every police officer.
   /911 <text>            calls the police and medics (the phone menu also offers it).
   /sms <text>            sends a text message from a phone you carry.
   /hangup                ends a phone call you are on.
   Using a phone item (Motorola RAZR, Sony Ericsson, Nokia 6820) opens its menu: call a player, answer, check status. A phone needs a Phone
   Subscription or a Phone Prepaid Card to be charged against. Plain chat is heard by the players within the talking range only; with arp_voice 1 so
   is voice. A line that starts with ( ( ... ) ) or /ooc is out of character.

 4. POLICE COMMANDS (job access letter a)
 ----------------------------------------
   /cuff                  as above; cops cuff other players with no item. A cuffed player drops his weapons, walks at half speed, cannot attack,
                          use, or jump, and is marked by a red glow and, over his head, a handcuff symbol (arp_h9_cuffsprite).
   /fine <amount>         sends a fine order to the player you look at (200 units): a menu offers him Pay or Refuse; the fine is taken from his
                          wallet and bank. He must have at least that much in the wallet and bank together.
   /com <text>            the police radio.
   jail <jail name>       puts the player you look at (200 units) in the named jail of the map's jailmod.ini.
   jailmodmenu            the jail menu: put the player you look at in a jail, free a jailed player.
   Search: the use key (E) on a handcuffed player opens the search window (arp_h9_search).

 5. PROPERTY COMMANDS (the door you look at, 200 units)
 -------------------------------------------------------
   A property is a door or group of doors with an owner, a price and a lock. Only the owner can use these.
   /sell <price>          puts the property up for sale at that price; /sell 0 (or no price) takes it off sale.
   /lock                  locks or unlocks the property's doors.
   /changename <name>     renames the property.
   /changeowner <name>    changes the owner's name that is written on it.
   /addaccess <user>      lets that player through the property's locked doors; /delaccess <user> takes it away again.
   /profit                takes out the money the property has earned (its shop sales times arp_profit_fraction).

 6. ARP ADMIN COMMANDS (console; job access letter z, or the server's console / rcon)
 -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Money and bank
     arp_createmoney <user> <n>    adds n to his wallet.        arp_removemoney <user> <n>   takes n from it.        arp_setmoney <user> <n>   sets it.
     arp_createbank <user> <n>     adds n to his bank.          arp_removebank <user> <n>    takes n from it.        arp_setbank <user> <n>    sets it.
   Items
     arp_createitems <user> <item> <n>   gives n of the item; <item> is a number or a part of the name (arp_itemlist lists them). A part that
                                         matches two items is refused ("Bug" is also "Bug Scanner"): use a longer part or the number.
     arp_removeitems <user> <item> <n>   takes n.                arp_setitems <user> <item> <n>   sets his count.
   Jobs and rights
     arp_setjob <user> <job id or name>   makes him that job at once ("Cadet" gives police rights; arp_joblist lists jobs).
     arp_employ <user> <job>              offers him the job, which he accepts or refuses in a menu.      arp_fire <user>   fires him.
     arp_setaccess <user> <letters>       sets his own rights (z = ARP admin); his job's letters are not touched.
     arp_setjobright <job> <letters>      sets a job's rights.
     arp_addjob <name> <salary> <letters>  adds a job (salary is paid every minute);  arp_deletejob <name>   deletes one.
   Properties and doors (look at the door)
     arp_addproperty <internal> <external> <owner> <authid> <price> <lock> <access> <profit>    makes a property.
     arp_setproperty <same arguments>   changes one (! keeps a value).   arp_deleteproperty   deletes the one you look at.
     arp_adddoor <internal>   hooks the door you look at to a property.  arp_deletedoor   unhooks it.   arp_propertyaccess <letters>   sets who may enter.
   Guns and other
     arp_addgun <weapon id> <ammo> <extra> <save 0/1>   puts a gun on the wall where you look (1 keeps it for the map).
     arp_config             opens the ARP admin menu: add the first ARP admin, change the database connection, run the map's SQL file.
     arp_getinfo            prints ARP's current state.
     arp_hudstate           prints who is sent the HUD panel and who the text HUD.
     arp_h9_inv_dump <user>, arp_h9_inv_check <user>   prints a player's inventory cells, and whether they agree with his items.
   Every admin command is written to ARP's log ("Cmd: ...").

 7. SERVER CONSOLE ONLY: THE DATABASE
 ------------------------------------
   arp_query <SQL>        runs one SQL statement and prints nothing (arp_query UPDATE arp_users SET access='z' WHERE authid='ID_...').
   arp_backup <name>      copies every ARP table into a backup (arp_sql_backup_db); the name has no underscore and no backup runs while one is running.
   arp_restore <name>     restores from a backup; it says to run arp_delete first.
   arp_delete [name]      DROPS TABLES. With no name it drops EVERY table of the ARP database; with a name, the tables of that backup. Back the file
                          data\sqlite3\arp.sq3 up before using it.

 8. OLD COMMANDS THAT SCRIPTS MAY STILL USE
 ------------------------------------------
   amx_additems / amx_delitems (server console), amx_employ, amx_setwallet, amx_setbank, amx_setjob (console): the names of the first Apollo release;
   they do what arp_createitems, arp_removeitems, arp_employ, arp_setmoney, arp_setbank and arp_setjob do. Use the arp_ names.

 9. LOOKING THINGS UP
 --------------------
   arp_help               every command ARP has registered, with its help text.
   arp_joblist            the jobs and their salaries.     arp_itemlist   the items and their numbers.      arp_plugins   the ARP plugins running.
   arp_queries, getclasses   debugging lists for the people who write ARP plugins.
   h9inv <op> <args>      what the inventory window sends; not for typing (COMMANDS.txt lists the ops).

 10. SWITCHES IN CONFIGS\ARP\ARP.CFG
 -----------------------------------
   Core         arp_startmoney 500 (wallet of a new character), arp_save_interval 300 (seconds between saves), arp_wallet_death 1, arp_police_access a,
                arp_medical_access b, arp_admin_access z (never empty it: ARP then lets everybody run admin commands), arp_goddoors 1, arp_godwindows 0,
                arp_falldamage 1, arp_log 1, arp_account_wait 30, arp_mapsql_autoload 1, arp_hud4_type.
   Talking      arp_ooc 2, arp_cnn 120, arp_advert 60, arp_capscom 1, arp_printmode 1, arp_message_range 1.0, arp_voice 1 (voice only within talking
                range), arp_message_log 0, arp_ooc_hudtitle.
   Jail         arp_jail_mode 1 (0 off, 1 leave the cell and be free, 2 a cop must free you), arp_jail_reconnect 1, arp_jail_death 1, arp_jail_distance.
   Shops        arp_ammofill_cost 50 (ammunition refill at a gun shop), arp_profit_fraction 0.25 (a property's share of what it sells).
   Needs        arp_hunger_enabled 1, arp_thirst_enabled 1.
   H9 additions (each has a stock "off"; docs\APOLLO_RP.txt has the table): arp_h9_vgui 1 (ARP's menus as H9 panels), arp_h9_itemhover 1,
                arp_h9_inv_window 1, arp_h9_inv_slots 32, arp_h9_inv_stack 20, arp_h9_inv_persist 1, arp_h9_credits 1, arp_h9_trade_range 250,
                arp_h9_trade_timeout 120, arp_h9_search 1, arp_h9_search_range 150, arp_h9_search_money 1 (take money in a search),
                arp_h9_restraints 1 (Cuffs, Tape and Rope exist; a change needs a map change), arp_h9_rope_length 150, arp_h9_cuffsprite 1 (the
                handcuff symbol over a cuffed head), arp_h9_toast 1 (the [ARP] chat lines as popups over the crosshair that fade: 0 chat as stock,
                1 popup instead of chat, 2 both), arp_h9_plaque 1 (what you look at, a property door, an NPC or a player, is a plaque on an H9 client: its name, owner
                or FOR SALE and the price, affiliation, and OPEN or SEALED; docs\APOLLO_RP.txt 2, docs\ARP_CONTENT.txt 9; the player's own switch is cl_h9_arphud_plaque).
   A server must be restarted fully (not an amxx reload) for a new cvar to register.
