===============================================================================
 TS MATCH MODE -- MAP ENTITIES
 What a map must name, what each thing is for, in build order
===============================================================================

 Companion to COMMANDS.txt. No custom entity classes are required anywhere:
 everything below is a STOCK Half-Life / The Specialists entity whose
 TARGETNAME carries the meaning -- and in one place a spawnflag. Names match
 case-insensitively, but always as a WHOLE name.

 The matching cvars live in ts_matchmode.cfg; the matching commands (amx_tsm_obj,
 amx_tsm_siege) are documented in COMMANDS.txt.

 ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  THE QUICK LIST -- everything to name in a map, in build order
 ------------------------------------------------------------------------------

   THE HEADLINE ANSWER DIRECTLY: the Breacher plants the SATCHEL on
   tsm_obj_1 through tsm_obj_9 -- brush entities (func_wall is usual)
   whose TARGETNAME is exactly the number, placed in the order they
   must fall. Everything else in this file supports that contract.

   1.  tsm_obj_1 .. tsm_obj_9
       any brush entity (func_wall is the usual; func_breakable allowed)
       => THE SIEGE OBJECTIVES -- the gates the attackers break through, in
          ascending number order.                            (section 1)

   2.  tsm_obj_N_reset  (one per objective N whose fall tears the map up)
       multi_manager, or anything Use-able
       => what puts YOUR scenery back after objective N falls.  (section 2)

   3.  tsm_eng_door  (EXACTLY this name)
       func_door (rotating or sliding)
       => a door only ONE class of ONE side can open.           (section 3)

   4.  tsm_breachdoor_1 .. tsm_breachdoor_8
       lists in ts_matchmode.cfg, holding door TARGETNAMES
       => breach OBJECTIVES: doors a Breacher ALWAYS forces ("Breached."),
          even with a master lock.                              (section 4)

   5.  (nothing to name)
       func_door and friends, matched by CLASS via tsm_breach_entities
       => movers the Breacher can force ONLY while truly locked. (section 4)

   And that is ALL. The round banners, the raid siren and its defenders'
   text, the team anthems, the Insurgent roar and the ambient music make NO
   demand on the map at all -- they are plugin-side.             (section 5)

   Three rules that rule everything:
   - The number of objectives STOPS AT THE FIRST MISSING DIGIT: naming
     tsm_obj_1, tsm_obj_2 and tsm_obj_4 makes a TWO-objective map.
   - An objective's name is EXACTLY tsm_obj_ plus one digit, 1 to 9:
     tsm_obj_10 does not exist, tsm_obj_1b is not an objective, and
     tsm_obj_1_reset is a relay (section 2), NEVER an objective.
   - Sections 1-3 and 5 are read at MAP LOAD; renaming or retuning them
     needs a map change. Only the section 4 cfg lists are re-read live.


 ===============================================================================
  1. SIEGE OBJECTIVES  --  targetname  tsm_obj_1 .. tsm_obj_9
 ===============================================================================

  WHAT IT IS FOR: the round's spine. The attacking side (tsm_obj_team, default
  team 2) breaks the chain IN ORDER up to nine deep; winning the round means
  felling every objective on it.

  HOW TO PLACE:

    Entity     : any brush entity; func_wall is the usual choice.
                 func_breakable is allowed -- read the caveat further down.
    Targetname : EXACTLY tsm_obj_N, one digit, 1 to 9, in attack order.
    Keyvalues  : target = your fall spectacle. It fires the instant the
                 objective falls, with the taker as the activator: an
                 env_shake, a target_cdz, a relay chain. Leave it out and the
                 objective still falls -- only without lights.

  HOW A FALL HAPPENS, IN ORDER:

    1. A player of the attacking side walks up to the FIRST standing
       objective (tsm_obj_range: 96 units).

    2. If their class matches tsm_obj_class (default "Breacher"; blank = any
       class of that side), aiming at it and HOLDING +USE starts the plant.
       The wrong side is told "That is yours to defend.", the wrong class
       "Only a <class> can set the charge."

    3. THE PLANT: a loading bar fills across tsm_obj_time seconds (10.0) on
       THE PLANTER'S OWN screen while the engine's satchel-reach clack
       (get_satchel.wav) rings spatially FROM THE OBJECTIVE'S SPOT,
       restarting as it ends. Letting go or looking away prints
       "Charge not set." and leaves the brush untouched.

    4. PLANTED: at the instant the hold completes, the server's own
       plant.wav (ts\sound) rings once from the spot and everyone reads
       "[MATCH] <name> planted the satchel on objective <n>." Any further
       hold on it answers "The satchel is already planted."

    5. THE FUSE: tsm_obj_fuse seconds (5.0) later the charge explodes --
       HL's own explosion sprite at the body's centre plus one stock boom --
       and THAT explosion is what destroys the objective: the body takes
       one piece of breach damage (a breakable dies with its own gibs), and
       a body that was never breakable is pulled from the world.

    6. THE FALL: the objective's target fires, the kill is announced, and,
       on the LAST objective, the round ends for side tsm_obj_team -- carried
       by a FINALE: three stock explosions at once world-wide and the screen
       shaking for the booms' combined length (never under 3 seconds). If
       tsm_obj_slay is 1, everyone is killed half a second later, outside
       the live round, so those deaths are not eliminations.

    7. THE ALARM: from the round's FIRST attack on anything the map defends
       -- a plant begun on an objective, or a Breacher forcing a breach door
       -- the siren (sound/bloblarm.wav) loops for the REST of the round,
       always exactly once however often the attack is pressed. Everyone
       hears it; the side under attack reads a line under screen centre in
       their own colour. Tuned by the tsm_alarm cvars.

  THINGS THAT ARE NEVER A SINGLE PRESS OF E:
    - No objective is destroyed by a single press. Planting is a HOLD; the
      one-press verb belongs to the breach doors (section 4).
    - ONLY a player matching the class gate (tsm_obj_class) plants. Every
      other attacking class has exactly one route to a fall -- gunfire, in
      a breakable body (next).

  IF THE BODY IS A FUNC_BREAKABLE:
    - Gunfire and grenades of ANY attacking class can break it, and the
      objective falls right there: the credit goes to the shooter.
    - A satchel already burning on it is reconciled: the body died to
      whoever took it, the fuse dies with it, and the fall keeps their name.
    - DEFENDERS CANNOT EVEN CHIP IT: their damage to an objective is cut to
      zero before it lands, so no defender can break their own gate.
    - Out-of-order destruction earns NOTHING: only the NEXT objective ever
      falls. A body destroyed ahead of its turn is rebuilt at the next
      round start from the cache (caveat below).

  THE BREAKABLE CAVEAT, READ ONCE BEFORE MAPPING:
    The plugin re-spawns a destroyed objective body at every round start from
    what it recorded at map load: entity class, brush model, health,
    spawnflags and fall target are replayed. Keyvalues the engine turns into
    HIDDEN class data at spawn are NOT (material, gib type on the entity
    itself). Keep an objective breakable an ORDINARY breakable, and put any
    spectacle on whatever its target fires instead.


 ===============================================================================
  2. OBJECTIVE RESET RELAYS  --  targetname  tsm_obj_N_reset
 ===============================================================================

  WHAT IT IS FOR: putting your map's own scenery back to how round 1 found it.
  A fall can demolish or open things beyond the objective entity itself; every
  NEW MATCH the plugin walks back from the top through what fell and fires a
  target named tsm_obj_N_reset for each of them, LAST first.

  HOW TO PLACE:

    Entity     : multi_manager, or anything Use-able.
    Targetname : tsm_obj_N_reset -- one for each objective N that damages the
                 rest of your map when it goes down.
    Keyvalues  : each entry <targetname>:<seconds> fires that restore. A
                 multi_manager fires entries IMMEDIATE unless given a delay
                 keyvalue -- a wall that needs two seconds to swing back
                 keeps its delay IN THE MANAGER, not in the plugin.

  A breakable objective body needs NO relay: it is deleted when it breaks and
  no relay can reach thin air -- the plugin re-spawns it from its own
  load-time cache (section 1's caveat). The relays only ever have to restore
  what the MAP's own explosion -- the target key -- demolished.


 ===============================================================================
  3. ENGINEER-ONLY DOORS  --  targetname  tsm_eng_door
 ===============================================================================

  WHAT IT IS FOR: a door exactly one class of exactly one side can open --
  the assist class of your choosing on a shortcut, entrance or flank.

  HOW TO PLACE:

    Entity     : func_door, rotating or sliding, both work.
    Targetname : EXACTLY tsm_eng_door.
    Spawnflags : nothing special; the lock is the plugin's Ham_Use gate,
                 not the mod's. If the map has none, amx_tsm_debug says
                 "eng doors: in_map=0" and the hook stays idle.

  THE GATE, enforced at every Use:
    - The use only counts when a LIVING player is behind the door, of the
      class named by tsm_eng_door_class (default "Combat Engineer"), and of
      the side named by tsm_eng_door_team (default 0 = either side; set the
      attacking side here so a defender cannot wave attackers through).
    - The class check matches the CLASS NAME, so renaming classes in
      ts_matchmode.cfg stays safe.
    - Nobody and nothing else passes: not another class, not a bot standing
      there, not a fired entity. The door answers people, nothing else.
    - A Breacher CANNOT force it. They are refused before the breach code is
      even consulted: "That door has no lock to break -- it reads who is at
      it." None of the map's own tricks can confuse that order.

  HEALTH CHECK: amx_tsm_debug prints how many tsm_eng_door doors the map
  has ("eng doors: in_map=n") and whether the Ham_Use hook is alive. Doors
  in the map plus ham_use still 0 means the hook is DEAD -- and the gate is
  then open to whoever walks up.


 ===============================================================================
  4. BREACHER DOORS
 ===============================================================================

  WHAT IT IS FOR: doors to be broken, not opened. A Breacher holding +USE
  within tsm_breach_range (96 units) force-opens a door. The plugin knows
  two families:

  (a) LOCKED STOCK DOORS, breachable by CLASS, no naming needed:
      every entity of a class named in tsm_breach_entities (default:
      func_door, func_door_rotating, func_wall_toggle, func_button) whose
      "Use Only" spawnflag (256) has it locked for everyone. The Breacher's
      use CLEARS that flag, the doorway stays open for the rest of the
      round, and the centre-screen "Breached." callout flashes. An ordinary
      UNLOCKED door under the crosshair gets stock TS behaviour and prints
      no message at all. Add a map's own movers -- or the MASTER locking a
      door -- to that list.

      NOTE ON MASTER LOCKS: a door whose lock is a master (multisource
      chain) cannot be forced directly -- the lock lives in the door's own
      private data, which a plugin cannot reach. Either put the master (or
      the trigger feeding it) in tsm_breach_entities and breach THAT, or
      name the door on a list (below).

  (b) DECLARED BREACH TARGETS, in ts_matchmode.cfg, not in the map:

        tsm_breachdoor_1 ""    (through tsm_breachdoor_8)

      Each holds a comma or space separated list of TARGETNAMES of doors
      that count as breach objectives for this map, for example:

        tsm_breachdoor_1 "gate_main,gate_side"
        tsm_breachdoor_2 "gate_yard"

      A door named on a list is treated as breachable EVEN IF its lock is
      a master chain, and +USE on it always breaches (and therefore prints
      "Breached."), whatever its spawnflags say.

  (c) NAMED BREACH DOORS, in the map, no list needed:

      a door whose targetname begins with tsm_breach_ (tsm_breach_1,
      tsm_breach_drive ...) is a breach door on every server, whether or
      not it runs a map config. Give it a targetname and no Use Only flag:
      a named door opens for nobody on touch or +USE, and the Breacher's
      +USE forces it. h9_siege_lift's are named this way.

  EVERY BREACH FIRES: the door itself, and everything the door targets in
  the same frame -- so a shell door firing the real mover opens the whole
  doorway, including paired double doors that each target the other.


 ===============================================================================
  5. WHAT THE PLUGIN PLAYS WITH NO ENTITY AT ALL
 ===============================================================================

  Nothing below needs a trigger, a relay or a name -- a map that declares
  none of it still gets all of it:

    - THE ROUND-START BANNERS: tsm_obj_msg_1 and tsm_obj_msg_2 in the cfg,
      one mid-screen read per side, team-coloured.
    - THE RAID SIREN and the defenders' alarm text (section 1's rule 7),
      from the round's first attack.
    - THE TEAM ANTHEMS: one team stinger per side one second into every
      round, locally (files in ts\sound\start; tsm_startsound 0 mutes).
    - THE INSURGENT ROAR: four seconds after the Insurgents' anthem, the
      monster's cry (ts\sound\mutantroar.wav) locally to TEAM 2 ONLY --
      the other side and bystanders never hear it.
    - THE AMBIENT MUSIC QUEUE (ts\sound\music; tsm_music per player).


 ===============================================================================
  6. TESTING -- FOR THE MAPPER
 ===============================================================================

   amx_tsm_obj            -- how many objectives the map has and their state
   amx_tsm_obj <n>        -- mark objective n already fallen
   amx_tsm_obj reset      -- put them all back

  The SIEGE DEBUG SUITE goes further: every stage of the flow with NO
  tsm_obj entities in the map at all (full details in COMMANDS.txt):

   amx_tsm_siege          -- the whole siege state, printed
   amx_tsm_siege_spawn    -- a synthetic objective where you look
   amx_tsm_siege_plant    -- force-complete a plant (the exact real path)
   amx_tsm_siege_fuse     -- set the planted charge off at once
   amx_tsm_siege_alarm    -- the raid siren, forced on
   amx_tsm_siege_reset    -- everything back to zero
