ARP SIEGE AND TOWN CONTROL -- how it works
==========================================

STATUS: DESIGNED. NOT IN THE GAME YET.
Phase 1 (the siege itself) is being built. This page says what it will do, so you can check it when it lands, and it is
updated with the build. docs\ARP_H9_ADDITIONS.txt lists everything else H9 added to Apollo RP; the siege is the one thing in
it that is not built. Where a line says "default", it is the plan: a "no" is one cvar or one line of siege.ini.

1. THE IDEA
-----------
The hive has three towns: Alexandria (key al), Paradise City (pc) and Xenon City (xe). Each is held by one side: the Ministry
(msr), the Insurrection (ins) or the Proletarians (pro). Today the Ministry holds all three. An admin opens a siege on a town.
While it lasts, any player can take the town for his own side by holding E on a console in the Ministry's detention area. The
side that holds a town owns its Ministry buildings, its name on the plaques and the money in its treasury.
A player cannot start a siege. Only the server console, rcon or an ARP admin (if allowed) can. That is on purpose: the admins
decide when a town can change hands.

2. A SIEGE, FROM THE COMMAND TO THE END
---------------------------------------
 1. The admin types  arp_h9_siege "pc"  ("al", "pc", "xe" or "all"). Or  arp_h9_siege_start pc 30  for a siege that ends by
    itself after 30 minutes.
 2. The town is in siege at once. A chat line says so. The siege mode's siren (bloblarm.wav) loops for everybody for as long as it
    lasts, and a "BREACH IN PROGRESS" banner shows every 20 seconds.
 3. The roaming citizens (the TSRP bots, 32 of them) turn into insurgents. They leave insurgent players alone and shoot
    everybody else.
 4. Every Ministry and Civil Protection door of that town opens to anybody who presses E: gates, offices, armouries, labs. The
    prison cells, the Paradise lift screen and the Throne Room stay shut.
 5. In the Ministry's detention area of that town the command console wakes up: it glows orange and has an orange ring on the
    floor.
 6. A player whose side does not hold the town holds E on the console for 30 seconds under an orange bar. A civilian can do it
    too: he takes the town for the Proletarians. The first to finish takes the town for his side and becomes its claimant.
 7. That ENDS the siege. The siren stops, the insurgent citizens become civilians again, the doors that were left open shut again,
    and the doors now answer to the new holder's cop jobs, its leaders and the claimant, not the old holder's.
A siege also ends if the admin types  arp_h9_siege_end pc , sets  arp_h9_siege "0" , the minutes run out, or the map changes.
A siege is never saved. The holder is.

3. WHERE THE THREE CONSOLES ARE
-------------------------------
Each stands in the Ministry's detention area of its town. The spots are data (configs\arp\maps\h9_tsrp_hive\siege.ini): if one is
wrong, it is one line.
 * ALEXANDRIA: the Ministry's Detention Facility 07 on the lower level. Ride a lift down (the funicular from the Paradise portal,
   the other lift from the Xenon portal) and walk to the "MSR DET FAC 07" gate. Ed's Steak House is on the same street. The console
   is in the big hall behind the gate, near its west wall, facing the gate.
 * PARADISE CITY: the Ministry of Defense Facility, across the street from the second bank kiosk (the one with the rotating BANK
   sign). The console is in the middle of the hall behind the gate.
 * XENON CITY: the Civil Protection post at the north end of the concourse, the lowest stop of the main elevator. The console is in
   the front hall behind the CP gate, near the Licensing Clerk.
Known limit: a player who comes through the Paradise portal lands where no walking route reaches Xenon's concourse. He has to
come round through Alexandria's portal to reach the Civil Protection post.

4. WHAT OPENS AND WHAT STAYS SHUT
---------------------------------
A "cop property" is a state property (no owner, price 0) sealed to a cop letter: the Ministry's and the Civil Protection's
posts, armouries, offices and labs. During a siege their doors open to everyone in the sieged town. They shut again when the
siege ends. Lifts and map doors that are not properties work as the map makes them.
Stay shut during a siege: the eight cells (an open cell would free its prisoner), the Paradise lift screen and the Throne Room.
arp_h9_siege_doors_scope 1 makes a siege open every town's doors instead of one town's.

5. WHO CAN TAKE A TOWN
----------------------
Any player whose side does not hold it. A civilian counts as a Proletarian. Only one claim is made per siege: the first to fill
the bar. A bot cannot claim or defend: bots press no buttons.

6. AFTER A CLAIM
----------------
 * The town's holder is saved in the database (the faction column of the treasury table, plus a log of who gave which town to
   whom). It stays after a restart.
 * The plaques and the HUD name the new holder: "Ministry of Defense", "Insurgent Headquarters" or "Labor Protection", with a
   "Held by:" line on the screen.
 * The doors answer to the new holder's cop jobs and leaders, and to the claimant. The old holder's do not open them.
 * The town's treasury, and what is in it, goes with the town to the new holder.
An admin can also hand a town to a side without a siege: arp_h9_siege_hand <town> <msr|ins|pro> [player], when
arp_h9_town_hand is 1.

7. THE PROLETARIAN COP JOBS
---------------------------
The Proletarians had no cop jobs. They get eight, under the name "Consolidated Labor Protection" (plaques say "Labor Protection"):
Security Associate, Patrol Officer, Senior Officer; Shift Supervisor, Site Supervisor; Security Manager, Regional Director, Chief
of Protection.
 * They open the doors of a town the Proletarians hold, as the Ministry's and the Insurrection's cops open theirs.
 * They are not police: no cuffs and no jail menu (that needs the Ministry's letter, which would make them Ministry). Taking one of
   these jobs moves nobody to another team.
 * Who hires them, with arp_employ: an admin; anybody given a "p", "pq" or "pqr" job right with arp_setjobright; and the claimant
   of a town the Proletarians hold, while he is its claimant (arp_h9_holder_hire).
 * They pay 20 to 90 credits like the other ranks. One pay.ini line each changes that. The unemployed stay at 0.

8. JOINING AND LEAVING A SIDE
-----------------------------
There is no recruiter NPC. Everybody starts as a Proletarian. Accept an arp_employ offer for a Ministry or Insurrection job and you
are on that side at once, because a player's team follows his job. /unemploy takes you back to Proletarian.

9. THE SWITCHES
---------------
Each feature has its own cvar. Plugin default = off (stock). The second number is what configs\arp\arp.cfg is planned to ship.
  arp_h9_siege "<towns>"          0 / 0   the gate. "0": no siege. "pc", "al,xe", "all". Live. Typing it starts sieges.
  arp_h9_siege_start/_end/_status the admin commands (server console, rcon, and ARP admins if arp_h9_siege_cmd is 1)
  arp_h9_siege_cmd                0 / 0   1: an in-game ARP admin may use the commands. The console and rcon always may.
  arp_h9_town_hand                0 / 1   the hand-over command
  arp_h9_claim, arp_h9_claim_secs 0 / 1, 30   the console and the length of the hold (5 to 600 seconds)
  arp_h9_siege_doors              0 / 1   a siege opens the cop doors
  arp_h9_siege_doors_scope        0 / 0   0: the sieged town; 1: every town while any siege runs
  arp_h9_siege_doors_close        0 / 1   the doors a siege opened shut again at the end
  arp_h9_holder_doors             0 / 1   the doors follow the holder
  arp_h9_holder_names, _hud       0 / 1   the plaque words and the "Held by:" line
  arp_h9_holder_hire              0 / 1   the claimant may hire the Proletarian cop jobs
  arp_h9_cops_pro                 "o" / "o"   the Proletarian cop letter
  arp_h9_siege_alarm              0 / 1   the siren and banner (_vol 0.3, _every 20, _msg "BREACH|IN PROGRESS|{town}", _scope 0)
  arp_h9_siege_bots               0 / 1   the citizens turn insurgent during a siege (needs arp_teamplay 1 and the TSRP bots on)
Off is clean for each: nothing half-running, and what a feature saved (the holders, the log) is kept.

10. NOT IN PHASE 1
------------------
 * The treasury terminals in each detention area, where leaders check, add and withdraw the town's money (an H9 panel).
 * Paid tax going into the treasury, and eviction by the leaders.
 * Guards at the posts, and the drug inspection (contraband is drugs other than cigarettes).
 * Forced models by faction (arp_h9_forcemodel, with a list of allowed models for each).
 * Posts that must fall before the console wakes.
 * A way to start a war from inside the game, and a price for it.

11. WHAT ONLY A GAME CAN SHOW
-----------------------------
The feel of holding E, the orange glow and ring, the siren, the plaque words, and the fight at the gates. The engineers' test
server has bots only, and a bot cannot press E.

12. THE PLUGINS AND FILES (planned)
-----------------------------------
ARP_Siege.sma (the switch, the holders, the claim, the end), ARP_SiegeClaim.sma (the consoles), ARP_SiegeDoors.sma (the doors rule,
the plaque words, the hiring right), ARP_SiegeAlarm.sma (the siren and the bots), include\ApolloRP_H9Siege.inc, and
configs\arp\maps\h9_tsrp_hive\siege.ini (the console spots, the names, the shut doors). Two small hooks go into ARP_Core and
ARP_Commands, and the siege mode's siren needs no change to ts_matchmode.
