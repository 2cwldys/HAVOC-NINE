APOLLO RP ON HAVOC 9: EVERYTHING H9 ADDED TO IT
================================================

This page lists every addition H9 made to Apollo RP (ARP), the roleplay mod of the maps h9_tsrp_hive and
h9_tsrp_newbyzantium. It is for an admin or a tester who wants to know what to look for.

"Stock ARP" is Apollo RP as its authors made it, before H9. Every feature H9 added has a switch with a stock "off", so a
server can play stock ARP (docs\APOLLO_RP.txt, section 10, says what each switch gives when it is off). Fixes of faults
(section 7) and the data files of H9's two maps (.sql, npcs.ini, zones.ini, pay.ini, propclip.ini) need no switch.

HOW TO READ AN ENTRY
  After each title, in square brackets, is where the detail is. ORIGIN is apollo-rp\ORIGIN.txt; the number is its item.
  What      what the addition is, in one sentence.
  You see   what a player or an admin sees, or types.
  Switch    the cvar that turns it off. A cvar is a setting you type in the console: a name, then a value. "plugin" is
            the value the plugin has when no file sets it. "arp.cfg" is the value configs\arp\arp.cfg ships. "0 = stock"
            is the value that gives stock ARP. "live" means a change counts at once, "map change" that it counts at the
            next map load. A player's own switch (cl_h9_...) follows.
  arp.cfg is configs\arp\arp.cfg in the game folder. Its master is apollo-rp\base\addons\amxmodx\configs\arp\arp.cfg.
  build.bat copies it, and ARP reads it again at every map load.

1. ROLEPLAY MENUS AND HUD
-------------------------

1.1  ARP'S MENUS AS H9 PANELS  [docs\H9_VGUI.txt 1e; ORIGIN 14, 29]
     What     Apollo RP's menus are drawn as H9 panels, on H9's purple art.
     You see  /menu, /items, the shops, the bank, the ATM, the doctor, the gun shop, the phone, the jail menu, fines
              and job offers, as panels with the same number keys. Other players keep the old text menu.
     Switch   arp_h9_vgui (plugin 1, arp.cfg 1, 0 = stock, live). A player: cl_h9_arpvgui 0. cl_h9_vgui_color r g b
              sets the colour of every H9 panel. The game's own h9_vgui 0 turns off every H9 panel.

1.2  H9'S ENTRIES IN /MENU  [docs\APOLLO_RP.txt 2; ARP_H9Menu, a plugin of H9's own]
     What     a small plugin adds four entries to the ARP client menu (/menu): Inventory, My money and job, Jobs on
              this map and Commands.
     Switch   none. The plugin is in plugins.ini. Without it /menu is empty, as in stock ARP.

1.3  THE HUD AS PANELS  [docs\APOLLO_RP.txt 2; docs\H9_VGUI.txt 1e; docs\ARP_HUD_CHECKS.txt; ORIGIN 21]
     What     ARP's four HUDs are H9 panels, not AMX Mod X text: the main one at the top left, the needs, the
              out-of-character chat and the short notices.
     You see  a purple panel at the top left with the server's title, Credits (the wallet), the bank, the payday
              countdown, the salary, the job and the zone. Every other player gets the old text HUD. An admin types
              arp_hudstate to see who is sent a panel, and why.
     Switch   arp_hud_panel (plugin 1, no arp.cfg line, 0 = everybody gets the text HUD, live) and arp_hud_refresh
              (plugin 10 seconds, 5 to 60, live). A player: cl_h9_arphud 0, and one switch for each part:
              cl_h9_arphud_left, _top, _side, _notice, _title, _wallet, _bank, _payday, _salary, _job, _status,
              _extra, _needs, _plaque, _icon and _shrink. cl_h9_arphud_list prints them.

1.4  THE PLAQUE: WHAT YOU LOOK AT SAYS WHAT IT IS  [docs\APOLLO_RP.txt 2; docs\ARP_CONTENT.txt 9; ORIGIN 41, 47]
     What     a plate in the middle of the screen says what the door, NPC or player under the crosshair is.
     You see  A property door: the building's name, OWNER (his name and job; STATE PROPERTY, which nobody can buy; or
              FOR SALE and the price), AFFILIATION, OPEN or SEALED (locked), and a red RESTRICTED line when you may
              not use it. An NPC: its name, its ROLE (SHOP, BANK, DOCTOR, GUN SHOP or ATM) and the hint "Press use (default
              e) to use", and never an owner. A player: his name, job and affiliation. Other games get the old text
              with an Affiliation line added.
     Switch   arp_h9_plaque (plugin 1, arp.cfg 1, 0 = stock text, live). The panel also needs arp_hud4_type 2
              (plugin 1, arp.cfg 2). A player: cl_h9_arphud_plaque 0.

1.5  A DROPPED ITEM NAMES ITSELF  [docs\APOLLO_RP.txt 2; docs\ARP_INVENTORY_WINDOW.txt 4; ORIGIN 28]
     What     looking toward an item on the floor tells you what it is.
     You see  "Soda x3" from 120 units, and "Press use (default e) to pick up" from 50 units. An H9 client draws
              the item's picture above that banner.
     Switch   arp_h9_itemhover (plugin 1, arp.cfg 1, 0 = stock, which says nothing, live). A player:
              cl_h9_arphud_icon 0 leaves the picture out.

1.6  THE ITEM'S PICTURE OVER A DROPPED PACK  [docs\APOLLO_RP.txt 2; modelling\arpicons\README.txt; ORIGIN 28]
     What     a small picture of the item floats over each dropped pack (the bundle a dropped item makes on the floor).
     You see  everybody who sees the pack sees it, turned toward him and readable in a dark corridor. At most 64
              stand at a time, and the oldest goes first. An item with no picture in the list has none.
     Switch   arp_h9_itemicon (plugin 1, arp.cfg 1, 0 = stock; a change to 1 needs a map change, a change to 0
              counts within 5 seconds), arp_h9_itemicon_max (64, 0 to 128, live) and arp_h9_itemicon_scale (0.6;
              1.0 is 32 units across; live for the next pack).

1.7  THE PURPLE GLOW OF A DROPPED PACK  [docs\APOLLO_RP.txt 2; ORIGIN 28]
     What     a pack that has just been dropped glows purple for 12 seconds, so it is easy to find in the dark.
     You see  a purple light over the pack that pulses every second and a half and fades by itself. A pack the
              map placed never glows.
     Switch   arp_h9_itemglow (plugin 1, arp.cfg 1, 0 = stock, live), arp_h9_itemglow_time (12 seconds, 0 to 600),
              arp_h9_itemglow_radius (200 units, 40 to 600) and arp_h9_itemglow_color ("180 60 255": red, green, blue).

1.8  POPUPS FOR ARP'S CHAT LINES  [docs\APOLLO_RP.txt 10; ORIGIN 40]
     What     the chat lines that start with [ARP] show as popups over the crosshair: up to three at a time, held
              4 to 7 seconds and then faded, a refusal in a warm colour.
     Switch   arp_h9_toast (plugin 1, arp.cfg 1; 0 = chat only, as stock; 2 = both; live).

1.9  CHAT BUBBLES AND /ME ACTIONS  [docs\CHAT_BUBBLES.txt 6; docs\ARP_COMMANDS.txt 3; ORIGIN 40]
     What     on an H9 client what a player says is drawn in a box over his head (the speaker your crosshair is on).
              On a roleplay map his /me action is drawn there too, in red.
     Switch   no ARP cvar. The bubbles have h9_floatchat_* on the server and cl_h9_chatbubble_* for a player.

1.10 NO NAMES OVER PLAYERS ON A ROLEPLAY MAP  [docs\H9_VGUI.txt 4j; the match mode plugin]
     What     the game's own names over players (red for anyone, since there are no sides) are left out. A player
              is known by what the plaque says of him.
     Switch   arp_h9_hidenames (plugin 1, ts_matchmode.cfg 1, not arp.cfg; 0 = the game's names; live within a
              second). A player: cl_h9_aimplaque 0 draws them regardless.


2. MONEY AND PROPERTY
---------------------

2.1  CREDITS AND THE CREDIT SIGN  [docs\ARP_INVENTORY.txt 7; docs\APOLLO_RP.txt 9; ORIGIN 31, 33]
     What     money is worded as credits and shown with the cent sign.
     You see  "1,250 credits" in the windows and messages, and "Credits:" on the HUD where stock ARP says "Wallet:".
              An H9 client with the inventory window shows the cent sign in place of "$" in chat, the console, the
              HUD and the H9 menus. The logs and the text menus keep "$".
     Switch   arp_h9_credits (plugin 1, arp.cfg 1; 0 = cash and "$", as stock; live).

2.2  THE PROPERTY TAX  [docs\APOLLO_RP.txt 11; docs\ARP_TAX_CHECKS.txt; ORIGIN 42]
     What     every property that has an owner costs its owner money every 15 minutes (a toll).
     You see  say /tax: what each of your properties costs at a toll, what it owes, how long it has, and the minutes
              to the next toll. At the shipped percent a property that cost 3,600 costs 4 a toll (16 an hour), and
              one that cost 110,000 costs 110 (440 an hour).
     Switch   arp_h9_tax (plugin 1, arp.cfg 1, 0 = stock: nobody is charged), arp_h9_tax_minutes (15; 1 to 10080),
              arp_h9_tax_percent (plugin 1.0, arp.cfg 0.10: 0.4 percent of the price an hour) and arp_h9_tax_flat
              (0). All live.
     Admin    arp_h9_tax_dump prints the clock and the last toll. arp_h9_tax_value <property> <price> sets a tax base.

2.3  PAYING THE TAX BY HAND: /RENEW  [docs\APOLLO_RP.txt 11; ORIGIN 44]
     What     a toll adds to what a property owes, and the owner pays it himself.
     You see  look at a door of your property and say /renew (or /paytax). A menu shows what it costs and owes, the
              tolls left before the server takes it back, your bank and wallet, and the keys: 1 pay (the bank first,
              then the wallet), 2 automatic payment on or off for that property, 0 close.
     Switch   arp_h9_tax_manual (plugin 0, arp.cfg 1; 0 = every owner is charged from the bank at once and what the
              bank cannot pay is forgiven; live).

2.4  EVICTION BY THE SERVER, AFTER 72 HOURS  [docs\APOLLO_RP.txt 11; docs\ARP_TAX_CHECKS.txt; ORIGIN 44]
     What     the server takes back a property that has gone unpaid for 288 tolls, which is 72 hours.
     You see  the owner is warned at half and at three quarters of his time. A property taken back is for sale again
              at the price its owner paid, open or sealed as it was. The ex-owner is told at once, or in his next
              login popup. Do not lower a limit while anything is owed: every property already over the new limit
              is taken back at the next toll, with no warning.
     Switch   arp_h9_tax_evict (plugin 0, arp.cfg 1; 0 = never; live), arp_h9_tax_evict_tolls (plugin 96 = 24 hours,
              arp.cfg 288 = 72 hours, 0 = no limit of this kind) and arp_h9_tax_evict_debt (0 = no limit of this
              kind). It needs arp_h9_tax_manual 1.

2.5  THE TOWN TREASURY  [docs\APOLLO_RP.txt 11, the shops' profit and the town's treasury; ORIGIN 45]
     What     the profit a taken-back property earned goes to the treasury of its town.
     You see  nothing in the game. An admin types arp_h9_tax_towns for each town's faction (who runs it) and money.
              The hive has three towns (Alexandria, Paradise City, Xenon City). Any other map is one town. Nothing can
              spend a treasury yet.
     Switch   arp_h9_tax_treasury (plugin 0, arp.cfg 1; 0 = the profit stays with the property, as at any sale; live).
              It needs arp_h9_tax_manual 1 and arp_h9_tax_evict 1.

2.6  SHOP PROFIT FOR A PROPERTY  [docs\ARP_CONTENT.txt 6; ORIGIN 45]
     What     a quarter of each sale at nine of the hive's sixteen shops is added to the property in front of it.
     You see  the property's owner says /profit while he looks at one of its doors, and takes the money.
     Switch   arp_profit_fraction (a stock cvar, 0.25; 0 = off). The links are lines in the hive's npcs.ini. New
              Byzantium has none.

2.7  PAY: UNEMPLOYED PAYS 0, PAY.INI AND A PERCENT  [docs\APOLLO_RP.txt 8; docs\ARP_CONTENT.txt 3; ORIGIN 49]
     What     a map's pay.ini sets what a job is paid, and one percent scales every salary. Unemployed is paid 0.
     You see  an Unemployed character's HUD says Salary: $0/hr and his bank does not move at payday. A Citizen is
              paid 10 every 60 seconds ("/hr" is upstream's word for it), into the bank.
     Switch   arp_h9_pay_percent (plugin 100, arp.cfg 100; 100 with no pay.ini = stock; live) and the map's pay.ini
              (a file read when the map loads; one line such as "Citizen" 12).
     Admin    arp_h9_pay_dump lists each job's row, its pay.ini line, what it is paid, and what ARP_Core (the main
              plugin) says it is paid.


3. NEEDS
--------

3.1  HUNGER AS A NEEDS PANEL  [docs\APOLLO_RP.txt 2; ORIGIN 26]
     What     hunger, which stock ARP already had, is a row with a bar in a "Needs" panel.
     You see  at the top right: Hunger from 0 to 100, with a bar that is green at 0, yellow half way and red from
              75, and marks at 75 and 90. From 90 a red "Starving" row shows, and there is no salary, as in stock ARP.
     Switch   arp_hunger_enabled (plugin 1, arp.cfg 1; 0 = no hunger; live). A player: cl_h9_arphud_needs 0.

3.2  THIRST  [docs\APOLLO_RP.txt 2; ORIGIN 27]
     What     a second need beside hunger.
     You see  a Thirst row under Hunger. It rises about a point every 45 seconds. Only a drink quenches it: Soda by
              35 and Milk by 30. From 90 a red "Dehydrated" row shows. Thirst slows nobody, costs no salary and sends
              no message. A thirsty player may drink with no hunger to heal.
     Switch   arp_thirst_enabled (plugin 1, arp.cfg 1; 0 = no thirst, as stock; live).

3.3  THIRST IS KEPT  [ORIGIN 27, the last paragraph]
     What     a character joins again with the thirst he left with, as his hunger always did. A death sets both to
              0, and nothing counts the time he was away.
     Switch   arp_h9_thirst_keep (plugin 1, arp.cfg 1; 0 = every join starts at 0; read at each load and save). The
              table is arp_h9_thirst.


4. LAW AND INVENTORY
--------------------

4.1  THE INVENTORY IN CELLS, AND ITS WINDOW  [docs\ARP_INVENTORY.txt 1 to 5; docs\ARP_INVENTORY_WINDOW.txt; ORIGIN 31]
     What     a player's items sit in cells, 32 of them with up to 20 of a kind in a cell, and an H9 client shows
              them in a window worked with the mouse.
     You see  /items, /inventory, or the key you bind to cl_h9_inventory, opens a window of eight cells across. You
              click, drag to move, combine and swap, split a stack, tidy, use, drop, or show an item to a player.
              With no room, a pick-up takes what fits and leaves the rest in the pack, and a shop refuses before it
              takes the money. Every other player keeps the text menu, and the cell rules apply to him too.
     Switch   arp_h9_inv_window (plugin 1, arp.cfg 1; 0 = the text menu; live), arp_h9_inv_slots (32, 8 to 96; 0 = no
              limit, as stock) and arp_h9_inv_stack (20, 1 to 999; configs\arp\inventory.ini names a limit for one
              item). The last two are read once a second. A player: cl_h9_arpinv 0.

4.2  THE ARRANGEMENT IS KEPT  [docs\ARP_INVENTORY.txt 8; ORIGIN 35]
     What     where a player put his items is stored in the database, so he joins and finds his cells as he left
              them, as far as he still owns the items.
     Switch   arp_h9_inv_persist (plugin 1, arp.cfg 1; 0 = packed again at every join; live). The table is
              arp_h9_cells. What a player owns stays in ARP's own table.

4.3  THE EXCHANGE OF GOODS  [docs\ARP_INVENTORY.txt 6; ORIGIN 32]
     What     two players swap items and money in a window of their own.
     You see  press the use key (E) on another player. He accepts with E on you. Each side offers up to 12 cells and
              some wallet money (never the bank). Any change breaks both seals. When both have sealed, the server
              swaps all of it at once, or moves nothing. Never with a bot, or a player who is cuffed or in jail.
     Switch   arp_h9_inv_window, arp_h9_trade_range (250 units) and arp_h9_trade_timeout (120 seconds). All live.

4.4  THE SEARCH OF A HANDCUFFED PLAYER  [docs\ARP_INVENTORY.txt 9; ORIGIN 36, 37]
     What     a player can search one who is handcuffed or in jail and take his items and his money.
     You see  press E on him. A window shows his cells beside yours. Drag or double-click to take. TAKE 10, TAKE 100
              and TAKE ALL take his wallet money (never his bank). He is told in chat. It ends when the cuffs come
              off, when he dies or leaves, or when you go further than 150 units.
     Switch   arp_h9_search (plugin 1, arp.cfg 1; 0 = stock: "That player cannot make an exchange now."),
              arp_h9_search_range (150) and arp_h9_search_money (1; 0 = items only). All live.

4.5  THE MENU OF THE USE KEY ON A PLAYER  [docs\ARP_INVENTORY.txt 10; docs\ARP_INVENTORY_WINDOW.txt 7; ORIGIN 50]
     What     the use key on a living player opens a menu with a cursor, in place of the offer of an exchange. It is
              the plugin ARP_H9Ctx, which is H9's own.
     You see  the rows Trade, Search, Cuff or Free, Tie or Untie, Tape mouth or Remove tape, Fine (police), Give
              money, and Show license or card. A row that does not apply is greyed with its reason ("Bots cannot
              trade", "Cuff him first", "Police only"). The server decides what is allowed, and again at the click.
     Switch   arp_h9_ctxmenu (plugin 0, arp.cfg 1; 0 = the use key as before; live), arp_h9_ctx_range (150),
              arp_h9_ctx_cuff, _fine and _give (1; 0 leaves that row out), arp_h9_ctx_fine_amounts ("50 100 250 500
              1000") and arp_h9_ctx_give_amounts ("10 50 100 500 1000"). A player: cl_h9_ctxmenu 0.
              Admin: arp_h9_ctx_dump.

4.6  CUFFS, TAPE AND ROPE AS ITEMS, AND THE HANDCUFF SYMBOL  [ORIGIN 38, 39; docs\ARP_ITEMS.txt 5, 6, 9]
     What     three items that stock ARP listed but could not sell or use, and a symbol over a cuffed head.
     You see  Police cuff with /cuff and need no item. Anyone else uses one Cuffs. Whoever frees him is given it
              back, unless he is police. Tape gags a mouth (chat, team chat, and voice with arp_voice 1) until
              /removetape. Rope ties the player you look at to you: he is pulled along when he is further than 150
              units, and /untie frees him. A handcuff symbol floats over a cuffed player's head for everybody.
     Switch   arp_h9_restraints (plugin 1, arp.cfg 1; 0 = stock, where the shops' lines say BAD ITEM ID; map change),
              arp_h9_rope_length (150; live) and arp_h9_cuffsprite (plugin 1, arp.cfg 1; 0 = no symbol, as stock; a
              change to 1 needs a map change).

4.7  JAILS THAT SHUT A REAL CELL  [ORIGIN 11, 13]
     What     a jail can name its cell's own cage and wall panel, and jailing a player shuts the cage on him.
     You see  the four cells of the hive's Alexandria jail shut and open as a player is jailed and freed. Their
              panels are the doors of one locked property, h9_jail, that only police use.
     Switch   none. The map's jailmod.ini is data. A map with no jailmod.ini has no jails, and the plugin still runs.

4.8  THE PPSH-41 AND THE LASER RIFLE AS ITEMS  [docs\ARP_CONTENT.txt 5; ORIGIN 34]
     What     H9's two own guns are items in ARP.
     You see  the Black Market sells the PPSh-41 (4,500) and the Laser Rifle (8,000), with no licence asked. An
              admin can give them. Using the item gives the gun at once.
     Switch   h9_customweapons (the game's own cvar). With 0 the game cannot make them: the item gives itself back
              and says so.

4.9  THE ITEM CATALOGUE  [docs\ARP_ITEMS.txt 12]
     What     a list of every item, 95 of them (92 with arp_h9_restraints 0): its number, what using it does and
              which shop sells it for how much.
     You see  arp_itemlist in your console (pages of 30: arp_itemlist 30, 60, 90). An admin gives an item with
              arp_createitems "<player>" "<item>" <n>.
     Switch   none. tools\gen_items.py writes the catalogue from the sources. tools\check_items.py checks it against
              the sources and against a capture of arp_itemlist.


5. TEAMS, DOORS AND THE WORLD
-----------------------------

5.1  TEAM PLAY BY JOB  [docs\APOLLO_RP.txt 2 and 6 (check 8); COMMANDS.txt, arp_teamplay; ORIGIN 43]
     What     a roleplay map plays on The Specialists' team rules with three teams, each player on the team of his job.
     You see  the scoreboard has three groups: Middle Sea Republic (blue), Insurrection (red) and Proletarians
              (yellow). Police, medical and Lord Minister jobs are the Republic's. Insurrection jobs are the
              Insurrection's. Every other job, Unemployed and an Administrator are Proletarians. A player cannot pick
              a team: jointeam and the team panel answer "Your team follows your job". A change of job that changes
              his team moves him and costs a respawn of 5 seconds (press fire). Friendly fire is on.
     Switch   arp_teamplay (plugin 0, arp.cfg 1; 0 = stock, no team is touched; map change). It is read from arp.cfg
              or from the map's own mapconfig only. If someone types arp_teamplay 1 at the console on a running map,
              nothing changes: the server prints a short notice (three lines in the console, one in the log) saying
              so, and arp_teams says it too.
     Admin    arp_teams lists every player's team by his job, by the game and by his entity.

5.2  THE INSURRECTION AND THE CITIZENS  [docs\APOLLO_RP.txt 2 and 6 (check 8); docs\ARP_CONTENT.txt 3, 4; ORIGIN 43]
     What     the roaming citizens (bots) are Proletarians. While an admin's .insurgency lasts they are the
              Insurrection.
     You see  after .insurgency the citizens leave one by one and come back armed in another district, under
              Insurrection on the scoreboard, with Insurgency on their plaque. They fire on every player who is not
              on the Insurrection team. .civilians puts them back. A citizen that a player hurts fights back.
     Switch   arp_teamplay 1, and arp_h9_insurgent_access (plugin i, arp.cfg i; live), the access letter of the
              Insurrection's jobs and properties.

5.3  DOORS AND BREAKABLES  [ORIGIN 20]
     What     doors cannot be broken. Windows, vents, crates and other breakables can.
     Switch   arp_goddoors (plugin 1, arp.cfg 1, stock) and arp_godwindows (plugin 0, arp.cfg 0; stock was 1, and 1
              protects every breakable too). Both are read once as the map starts: map change.

5.4  ZONES AND THE LOCATION LINE  [docs\ARP_CONTENT.txt 7; ORIGIN 47]
     What     a map's zones.ini names the places of the map, and the HUD says which one the player is in.
     You see  "Location: Alexandria", "Paradise City" or "Xenon City" on the hive (one for each city, so one for each
              level: take a lift to change it), and "New Byzantium" on the other map. Unknown only where a map has
              no zone.
     Switch   arp_h9_zonefix (plugin 1, arp.cfg 1; 0 = the stock plugin; the zones are read at a map change, the
              touch of a trigger at once). It mends two faults of the stock Zones plugin: it stopped after the first
              zone of a file, so a player outside it read Unknown, and it blocked every map trigger (an invisible
              switch that opens a door), so the hive's automatic doors (Tartec's front doors and the Paradise Bank's
              glass doors) never opened.

5.5  SOLID PROPS  [docs\APOLLO_RP.txt 12; ORIGIN 48]
     What     an invisible solid box is put on props that the map left walk-through. It is the plugin ARP_PropClip,
              which is H9's own.
     You see  the hive's two bookshelves of the Lord Minister's throne room and two bookcases stop players, bots and
              most bullets. A shelf is not sure cover: some shots go through.
     Switch   arp_h9_propclip (plugin 0, arp.cfg 1; 0 = stock; live, looked at every second) and the map's
              propclip.ini (a file). tools\propclip_tool.py makes its lines and checks them against the map.


6. THE HIVE MAP'S DATA
----------------------

These are the data files of the two roleplay maps, in configs\arp\maps\<map>\. They need no switch. The numbers are the
hive's (h9_tsrp_hive) unless a line says New Byzantium (h9_tsrp_newbyzantium).

6.1  THE JOBS  [docs\ARP_CONTENT.txt 3 and 4; ORIGIN 19, 43]
     What     40 jobs on each map: Unemployed and five civilian jobs, the Ministry of Defense ladder from Cadet to
              Lord Minister (with the medical M- ranks), the Administrator, and the Insurrection's 14 ranks from
              Recruit to Liberator.
     You see  arp_joblist lists them. An admin gives one with arp_setjob <player> "Cadet", which also gives police
              rights. A new character starts with 500 in the wallet and no job pay (Unemployed pays 0).

6.2  THE NPCS, THE NEW ONES, AND THE NPC PLAQUE  [docs\ARP_CONTENT.txt 5 to 7; ORIGIN 19, 47]
     What     the hive has 24 NPCs and New Byzantium 32: shops, bank tellers, ATM droids, doctors, a gun shop,
              ammunition and licence counters, and the Ministry's quartermaster. Look at one and press the use key.
     You see  two were added later. Cafe Avalon Cantina is a Cantina shop in the bay of the wood counter at the west
              end of the hall under the plaza's east side. Two Bank Terminals are invisible NPCs that stand before
              the screens of the two BANK kiosks (the plaza's, and the south west's): use opens the teller's menu,
              and /withdraw and /deposit work while you face one. An NPC's plaque never shows an owner.

6.3  THE PROPERTIES AND THEIR DOORS  [docs\ARP_CONTENT.txt 9; ORIGIN 41, 46]

7. FIXES OF STOCK BUGS, AND WHAT MAKES ARP RUN ON H9XASH
--------------------------------------------------------

These have no switch, because each one mends a fault or lets ARP run on H9's game. The number is the ORIGIN item.

  1   ARP runs on roleplay maps only. On any other map it stands down and logs one line.
  2   A map's tables carry its name (arp_property_<map> and others), so one database serves every roleplay map.
  3   ARP loads the SQLite driver itself. The game's module list does not name it, and stock ARP would load MySQL.
  4   Every list sized for 32 players is sized for 128, and so are the buffers that fetch the player list.
  5,6 Two plugins each carried an ammunition routine that clashed with the game's. Both now use the game's own, and the
      weapon tables follow The Specialists' own weapon numbers.
  7   The assassin knife item notices damage the way the match mode does, and logs its first hit on each map.
  8   A database with no jobs no longer stalls the HUD and the hover line. ARP_Core, the main plugin, makes "Unemployed".
  9   Bots are neither loaded from the database nor saved to it. They share one id, so only the first could be saved.
  10  A task that carried a player as a number plus 32 carries him plus 128. A property's key holders are one bit
      for each of 128 slots, so slot 33 no longer holds slot 1's keys. A bug (a listening device) is kept for each
      pair of slots, and a player who leaves takes the bugs he planted.
  11  A map with no jailmod.ini has no jails, and the plugin runs. "jail <command>" numbers jails from 1.
  12  A HUD with nothing to show is cleared once and left alone. Before, ARP held all four of AMX Mod X's HUD text
      slots every second, and other plugins' HUD text had none left.
  16  Text that goes into a database query is escaped with the SQL module's own routine. A job name with a quote no
      longer breaks a query, and an owner's id is read at its full 35 characters.
  17  No HUD or hover line is sent to a bot. That stops "Datagram overflowed" in ts\logs\h9_overflow.log.
  19  A name that is inside another job's name (Cadet, M-Cadet) is the job you name: arp_employ <player> "Cadet"
      offers Cadet. A short name that matches several jobs no longer writes past the end of a list.
  20  arp_godwindows ships as 0: doors are protected, breakables can be broken (5.3).
  22  The lists ARP keeps (the /menu entries, a player's HUD lines, his items, speed changes) are emptied the right
      way. Before, the client menu, the HUD lines, the out-of-character chat and the item list stayed empty.
  23  A save of ARP's class data waits for every answer of the database. Before, it could destroy the data first and
      log "Invalid trie handle provided".
  24  The OOC ping is a new sound: a short two-step chirp and a high ping. The old one ships as ooc.wav.original in
      sound\arp, and copying it over ooc.wav brings it back.
  30  A drop makes the pack first and takes the items after, so none is destroyed. Using an item you no longer have is
      refused. The use key on a pack takes what fits and leaves the rest.
  42  The owner key. H9XASH ids are 35 characters ("ID_" and 32 digits). Two stock routines cut them to 32 and 33, so
      /sell, /lock, /changename, /changeowner, /addaccess, /delaccess and /profit told an owner "You are not the
      owner of this property." Both now keep the whole key (H9-arpowner.patch).
  47  The Zones plugin's two faults (5.4).

     What     H9XASH makes a player's id for each server, so a server that changes its address would have given every
              player a new character. A player whose game is linked to a havoc9.org account and proves it keeps his.
     You see  for up to 30 seconds after joining, the HUD says his account is loading. Then his own character loads.
     Switch   arp_account_wait (plugin 30, arp.cfg 30; 0 = the id alone, as before; read at each join).
