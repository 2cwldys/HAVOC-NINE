PPSH-41: ATTRIBUTION
====================

The PPSh-41 view model (v_ppsh41.mdl), player model (p_ppsh41.mdl) and world
model (w_ppsh41.mdl) are adapted from a 3D model published on Sketchfab:

  "PPSh-41" by Zillious
  Model:   https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/ppsh-41-5c8a64490fa747389e9cfcaecc69c88a
  Author:  https://sketchfab.com/zillious
  Licence: Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY), the licence listed on the
           model's page (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). It
           allows use and adaptation, including commercial use, with credit to
           the author and a note of what was changed. The model's page is the
           authority on its licence.

CHANGES MADE FOR THE SPECIALISTS / H9XASH

  - The mesh (12,394 triangles, one piece) was split into its parts and
    reduced to 2,078 triangles, to fit what a GoldSrc model holds.
  - The drum magazine, modelled facing along the barrel, was turned a quarter
    turn so that it sits across the gun as on the real weapon, and its back
    disc, which the source winds inward, was turned to face out.
  - The 4096 x 4096 physically based textures were baked into one colour
    picture of 512 x 512 (view model) and 256 x 256 (player and world models),
    with the steel darkened and tinted.
  - The model was fitted onto The Specialists' own AK47 models, so the hands
    and every animation are the game's: the drum follows the AK's magazine,
    the trigger its trigger, the charging handle its bolt.
  - None of the original file, nor its full-size textures, is included here.

FIRING SOUND

  '44-'45 (DH_WeaponSounds.uax), the Red Orchestra game, trimmed and faded.
