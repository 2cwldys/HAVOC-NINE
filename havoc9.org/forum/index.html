    THE RULES

    • Be decent. Argue with the post, not the person. No slurs, threats or harassment.
    • No cheats, no exploits for sale, no pirated content, no spam, no advertising.
    • Post in the category that fits; a moderator may move a thread.
    • Moderators can lock, move, remove and restore threads and posts, and turn off an account's posting for a while. Every such step is recorded.
    • See something wrong? Use REPORT under the post: only moderators see reports.

    WRITING

    • **bold**, *italic*, `code`; a line of ``` before and after a block of code; > at the start of a line for a quote; a blank line for a new paragraph.
    • Links: paste the address (https://...) or write [words](https://...). Links open in a new tab and are marked as written by members.
    • Pictures are links to pictures on other sites (imgur and a few other picture hosts, listed under FORMATTING when you write): put a picture link alone on its line, or write ![words](link). Nothing is uploaded to havoc9.org; the picture is shown from its own site, which sees readers' addresses as any website does. A post shows a limited number of pictures (FORMATTING says how many); the rest stay links. No HTML: everything else you type shows as typed. A title is up to 120 characters, a post up to 10,000.
    • You can edit a post for an hour after writing it, and remove your own posts at any time.

    WHAT THE FORUM KEEPS

    Your posts and threads, with when you wrote and edited them; your profile picture if you add one (under ACCOUNT, with its metadata such as camera and location removed); and reports you send, which only moderators see. No IP address is stored with a post. A removed post is hidden from readers but kept for the moderators. If your account is deleted, your posts stay, shown as from a deleted account; ask a moderator to remove any you want gone.
