COMMUNITIES

WHAT A COMMUNITY IS

A group of game servers run by one havoc9.org account, its owner, and the admins the owner names. Its players talk across all its servers and this page: .relay <text> in game chat, or the chat box here when signed in. In game, /find <name> or /where <name> says where a player is, this community's servers first, and portals on its maps lead to its other servers.

A server joins once it is enrolled with havoc9.org, which the H9 presence plugin does by itself, and its console's h9_community_claim code is entered here by the community's owner or an admin. A ban or a mute here holds on every server of the community. What a player hides with the switches on their ACCOUNT page stays hidden here too.
