MUSEUM

The original Havoc9 was one of the largest TSRP communities. Future dystopia. Ministry of Defense. Rule of RP. This page preserves that history.

// ARCHIVE 001

HISTORY

2005–2006

FOUNDING

Havoc9 was created after Cosmo and HavocPrime met on the TacoLand RP server. Deciding they could do better, they founded a community free of the typical administrator tyranny that plagued other TSRP servers.

Early members: Cosmo, HavocPrime, Target, SageOwl, Kill, Ian, Andy, Lenore, Kat (Whisper), Superlagg, Scott, Timmeh

2007

XENON CITY

The server transitioned to XenonCity and locked in the setting that defined it: a world 100–200 years in the future. An oppressive government. The Xenon City Civil Protection (XCCP). Fully developed AI and sentient androids available for roleplay.

2008

PARADISE CITY & THE MIDDLESEA REPUBLIC

Cosmo, SaderBiscuit, and Punch released ParadiseCity. The setting expanded into the Middlesea Republic — a massive power controlling most of Europe, North Africa, and the Mediterranean. The police force became the Ministry of Defense (MoD), led by a Lord Commander.

2009–2010

WESTMINSTER & LA 2033

A return to run-down urban dystopia on AHL_Westminster (The Warriors-style RP), followed by Cosmo’s LA_2033 (Esperanza) set in Los Angeles, 2033.

2011–2012

REVIVALS

Xalphox launched Havoc11 in 2011. Cosmo returned in 2012 with the Alexandria map — capital of the Middlesea Republic — marking the final lifecycle of the original Havoc9 community under Cosmo.

2017

THE LAST STAND

Aurora, MGZoltan (Flaks), Darkfire and others revived Havoc9 on Alexandria, later moving to a modified ParadiseCity. At one point the 27-slot server hit full capacity.

// ARCHIVE 002

THE WORLD

◈

THE MIDDLESEA REPUBLIC

A sprawling superstate controlling Europe, North Africa, and the Mediterranean. Its capital, Alexandria, stood as both a gleaming center of power and a powder keg of oppression.

◈

MINISTRY OF DEFENSE

The MoD replaced the earlier XCCP as the iron fist of the state. Hierarchical, heavily armed, and answerable only to the Lord Commander.

◈

RULE OF RP

Realism is the expectation. Stay in character, respect the world, and take your losses with humility. Being able to lose gracefully is part of good roleplay. Clique culture was discouraged — you could start with nothing, make friends, and rise.

◈

ANDROIDS & AI

Sentient machines were not just background flavor — players could fully roleplay as androids and droids in a world where artificial intelligence had already achieved true consciousness.

LEGACY

“Havoc9 encouraged players to abide by a Rule of RP with regard to CKs, deals, and acquisitions. From time to time, community staff would be known to forfeit battles or disputes in favor of members. This was unheard of in other communities.”

Havoc9 was never just another server. It was a deliberate rejection of the elitism and admin abuse that defined so much of the early TSRP scene. Players who started with nothing could band together, make friends, and climb.

The maps changed — XenonCity, ParadiseCity, Westminster, Esperanza, Alexandria. The faces rotated. The core idea stayed: a fairer table in a dystopian future.
