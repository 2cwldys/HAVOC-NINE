WHAT LINKING DOES

Your game then plays as this account on every H9 server: presence, your name in game, your picture on the scoreboard and the PLAYS IN GAME badge all follow. Nobody types or pastes an id: the game is given its player id here, once, and keeps it protected for its Windows user.

Your linked games are listed on your ACCOUNT page, each with REVOKE. A revoked game finds out the next time it starts, and gets an id of its own again. In the game, Unlink your Account in the main menu does the same from its side.
