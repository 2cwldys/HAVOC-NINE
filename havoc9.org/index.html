// THE MOD

WHAT IS HAVOC9

◈

PROGRESSIVE SIEGE

Mapped objective-based siege, limited lives. Take side routes, direct approaches, stage distractions, flank, and attack up to a maximum of nine objectives with nine siege classes. Destroy siege objectives to progress to victory. Time limit configurable by map author. Attackers must destroy siege objectives to win — not by enemy team elimination alone.

◈

TEAM ELIMINATION

Squad vs squad. Wipe the other team to win the round. Tight, high-stakes fights with no respawn safety net.

◈

ROLEPLAY

Full roleplay servers in the Havoc9 tradition. Realism is the expectation — stay in character, respect the world, and take your losses with humility.

◈

TEAM DEATHMATCH

Score for your side. Classic team deathmatch with The Specialists gunplay — fast rounds, constant pressure.

◈

DEATHMATCH

Free-for-all. Every player for themselves. Climb the frag board or get buried under it.

◈

CAPTURE THE UPLINK

Only one person from either team may hold the uplink. Whoever carries the uplink can contest and take zones for their team. Hold the core, claim every zone, and win.

◈

WAVE SURVIVAL

Survive against waves of mutant or insurgent hordes. You can become eliminated — they cannot.

◈

MECH WARRIOR

One officer a side on two lives, and as many mechs as your team can field on three lives each. Every bot pilots a mech. Elimination rounds, respawn waves and sudden death, as in team elimination.

◈

H9XASH CLIENT

A custom Xash3D-based client with H9-specific patches, modern tooling, and a clean path for both Windows and future web builds.

◈

OPEN SOURCE

Plugins, engine patches, model kit, and tools are public. Contribute models with SMAK, fix bugs, or help ship the next map.

FROM THE ORIGINAL

The original Havoc9 was one of the largest TSRP communities — future dystopia, Ministry of Defense, Rule of RP, and a reputation for fair play. This project carries that name and spirit forward as a modern, open-source mod: new modes, new tools, same expectation of realism and humility in defeat.

The full history, maps, ranks, and archive live in the Museum.
