ADMIN PANEL

Checking who you are…

Enrolled game servers (game servers with a havoc9.org key, so they can report who is playing and take part in communities), the ones waiting for approval among them, and H9-wide bans, mutes, kicks and announcements: COMMUNITIES, in its H9 ADMIN section.

HOW THIS WORKS

  • Every button here is checked again by the server; the panel only shows what your role may do.
  • Nobody acts on an account of their own role or above. Moderators act on players; admins on players and moderators.
  • Admins are made and unmade only with the admin command on the server, never here.
  • Reset password, delete, a role change, a suspension with no end, approving a name change, resetting or moving a player id, and adjusting a rank ask for your own password.
  • Every action goes into the audit log, which admins can read and which is kept for a limited time.
