INSTALLATION

  1. Download the H9XASH client for your platform.
  2. Extract the archive to a folder of your choice.
  3. Copy your legal Half-Life valve folder (and any other required game directories) into the client directory.
  4. Launch the client, then connect to a server from the Servers page.
