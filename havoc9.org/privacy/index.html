WHAT WE STORE ABOUT YOU

Last updated 15 November 2026. This is the privacy notice for havoc9.org and its services (the forum, accounts, the ladder, the server list and the web client).

WHO IS RESPONSIBLE

havoc9.org is a non-commercial fan project run by the Havoc 9 maintainers, who decide what is stored and why (the "controller" in the GDPR). For anything on this page, send a private message to an admin in the Havoc 9 Discord, or open an issue at github.com/2cwldys/HAVOC-NINE (never put private details in an issue; say you need a private reply).

WHAT WE STORE

  • Your account: the username you chose, and a salted hash of your password (never the password itself). We do not ask for an email address, a real name or a date of birth.
  • Staying signed in: one cookie holding a random token (only a hash of it is stored on the server), valid for 30 days at most and 14 days without use. It is needed for the site to work and is the only cookie. There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The forum: what you write (threads, posts, wall comments, your signature), your profile picture, your votes and any reports you make. Posts and profiles are visible to visitors.
  • Your game, if you link it: a hashed identifier of your game installation, the name you play under, your ranked results (rating, career level and experience, matches, kills), playtime and whether you are online on a server enrolled with havoc9.org. These show on the ladder, the forum and your profile.
  • Discord, only if you link it: your Discord user ID and display name, so the site can give you the registered role.
  • Admin actions: a log of what admins and moderators did to an account (who, what, when). It is kept 90 days.
  • Logs: the web server and the security tools see your IP address and browser details when you visit. They are used to stop abuse and attacks and are kept about 14 days (the service log for 30 days).

Game servers you join keep their own logs; that is the server operator's responsibility, not this site's.

WHY, AND ON WHAT BASIS

  • Running your account, the forum, the ladder and the server list, which you asked for by signing up (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Keeping the site and its players safe from abuse, spam and attacks (Art. 6(1)(f), our legitimate interest).
  • Linking Discord, which is your choice (Art. 6(1)(a), consent). Unlink it and we stop.

WHO ELSE SEES IT

  • Cloudflare sits in front of the site, so it handles every request (it sees the IP address and what you ask for).
  • Our hosting provider runs the server the site lives on.
  • Discord, only if you link it.
  • Other websites, when a post or page embeds them: forum posts can show pictures from a few image hosts (for example Imgur and Giphy) and the media page embeds YouTube videos (in its privacy-enhanced mode). Your browser then contacts those sites and they see your IP address. If you do not want that, do not open those pages. The site's own fonts and scripts come from this site, not from Google or any other third party.

These providers may process data outside the European Economic Area (for example in the United States), under the safeguards the GDPR allows, such as standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. We do not sell your data.

HOW LONG

  • Account data: for as long as your account exists. If you ask us to delete it, it is deleted, and your forum posts with it on request.
  • Sessions: 30 days at most. Admin action log: 90 days. Logs: about 14 to 30 days, as above.
  • Backups: daily copies are kept 14 days (7 for the encrypted off-site copy), so deleted data can remain in a backup for up to two weeks and is not restored afterwards.

YOUR RIGHTS

You can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to limit or stop how we use it, to move it to another service, and you can withdraw consent at any time. Contact us as above; we answer within one month. If you think we handle your data wrongly, you can complain to the data protection authority of the country where you live or work.

CHILDREN

The site is not aimed at children. If you are under 16, ask a parent or guardian before you make an account.

CHANGES

If this changes in a way that matters to you, the date at the top changes and we say so in the Discord.
