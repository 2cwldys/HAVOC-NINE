Want to tinker?

All of it is open — the engine patches, the plugins, the maps, this website. If you have ever wanted to change something in a game you play, this one will let you.

There is a toolkit for it that runs entirely on your own machine, if your machine can take it: a local model does the reading and the boilerplate with the project's real rules in front of it. No paid API, no account, nothing leaving your computer.

The CONTRIBUTE page has the setup and what it needs to run. None of it is needed to build, host or play.
