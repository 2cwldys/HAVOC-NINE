and install it. LM Studio is proprietary third-party software. If you accept that download you are
using it under its own application terms of service:

Those terms are between you and the makers of LM Studio. Nothing in this repository alters, restates or
grants anything in them, and no part of LM Studio is distributed with HAVOC 9. The scripts here only
speak to it over a local HTTP port (http://localhost:1234/v1 by default), the way any OpenAI-compatible

download inside LM Studio are a third thing again: each model carries its own license, several are
